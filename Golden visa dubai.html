What Is the UAE Golden Visa?

Dubai is the UAE's global business capital — home to the world's largest free zones, international finance, luxury real estate, and a booming entrepreneurial ecosystem. The UAE Golden Visa is a 10-year, self-sponsored residency that allows investors, entrepreneurs, skilled professionals, and outstanding graduates to live and work in Dubai independently — with no employer requirement, no travel restrictions, and the ability to sponsor family members including parents. For Dubai residents, the Golden Visa is processed through ICA with documentation coordinated through DED (Department of Economic Development) for mainland investors and the relevant free zone authority for free zone entrepreneurs.
