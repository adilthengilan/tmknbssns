What is CICPA?

Critical Infrastructure & Coastal Protection Authority

CICPA is the UAE government authority responsible for controlling access to critical infrastructure zones — including oil fields, refineries, pipelines, coastal facilities and industrial areas in Abu Dhabi.

Any individual or vehicle entering these protected zones must hold a valid CICPA pass. Tamkin Express handles the full application process so your team can access worksites without delay.
