Understanding CICPA

What is a CICPA Pass
and Who Needs One?

The Critical Infrastructure and Coastal Protection Authority (CICPA) is the Abu Dhabi authority responsible for protecting critical infrastructure and coastal areas in the emirate. A CICPA pass is a security clearance that lets employees and contractors enter protected sites such as oil and gas facilities, ports, pipelines and other restricted zones in Abu Dhabi.

Companies working on these projects usually need passes for every worker before they can start work on site. Missing documents, expired visas or incorrect company details are the most common reasons for delays, and a delayed pass can hold up an entire project team.

Requirements can differ from site to site and are set by the authorities, so Tamkin Express confirms what applies to your project first, then prepares and submits the application. We also help with the visa and permit side through our PRO services and Ministry of Labour services.

Documents Required

CICPA Pass Documents —
Typical Checklist

The documents below are the typical requirements for a CICPA pass application. Individual sites and project owners can ask for additional items, so we confirm the exact list for your case during the consultation.

Companies generally need an Abu Dhabi mainland trade licence to apply, and some sites also require sector approval, for example for oil and gas work. We check eligibility for your company before you apply. Need a licence first? See Ministry of Economy services.

Before applying, make sure the employee's residence visa and Emirates ID are valid, because an expired visa is one of the most common causes of delay. If visas need renewing first, our employment visa services can handle it alongside the pass.

For bulk applications we collect documents for the whole team in one structured batch, which keeps submissions consistent and easier to track.
