FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a CICPA pass?

Required to access critical infrastructure, ports, and restricted zones in UAE.

How long does CICPA processing take?

Standard 3–7 working days. Expedited processing available for urgent cases.

What documents are needed?

Passport, valid UAE visa, Emirates ID, trade license, and sometimes an NOC.

How long is a CICPA pass valid?

Typically 1–2 years. Must be renewed before expiry to maintain access.

Can companies apply for multiple passes?

Yes — bulk processing available for companies at discounted rates.

What if my application is rejected?

We review rejection reasons and guide you through reapplication with corrected docs.
