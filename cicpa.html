Frequently Asked Questions

What is a CICPA pass?

CICPA (Critical Infrastructure and Coastal Protection Authority) passes are required to access critical infrastructure, ports, and restricted zones in UAE.

How long does CICPA processing take?

Standard processing takes 3-7 working days. We offer expedited processing for urgent requirements.

What documents are needed for a CICPA pass?

Passport copy, valid UAE visa, Emirates ID, company trade license, and sometimes a No Objection Certificate from your employer.

How long is a CICPA pass valid?

CICPA passes are typically valid for 1-2 years and must be renewed before expiry to maintain access.

Can my company apply for multiple CICPA passes?

Yes, companies can apply for multiple passes for their employees. We offer bulk processing at discounted rates.

What if my CICPA pass application is rejected?

We review the rejection reasons and guide you through the reapplication process with corrected documentation.
