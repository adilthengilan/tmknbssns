What Does a PRO Do in Dubai?

A PRO (Public Relations Officer) in Dubai handles all interactions between a company and UAE government authorities — DED, MOHRE, ICA, EIDA, Dubai Courts, and free zone authorities (DMCC, JAFZA, DIFC). The PRO submits visa applications, renews trade licences, registers establishment cards, coordinates medical tests, and manages residency stamping. Without a PRO, Dubai companies must navigate these processes manually, risking delays, fines, and compliance failures.
