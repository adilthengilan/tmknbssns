How We Work

Our Process

A clear, structured app delivery process built for Abu Dhabi businesses — from first consultation to App Store launch, with no surprises and no delays.

01

Discovery & Scoping

We start with a free consultation — in person at our Musaffah M43 office or remotely — to understand your Abu Dhabi business, target users, and regulatory requirements. We produce a clear scope, timeline, and fixed estimate before any work begins.

02

UI/UX Design

Our designers create wireframes and high-fidelity app mockups aligned to your brand. Arabic RTL and English bilingual layouts are standard deliverables for Abu Dhabi apps targeting both UAE nationals and international audiences.

03

Development & Integration

We build in agile sprints with weekly updates. TAMM, UAE PASS, MOHRE, FTA, and UAE payment gateway integrations are core deliverables — handled as part of the main build, not as costly extras.

04

QA & Security Testing

Every app is tested across the top Abu Dhabi market devices, and undergoes a full security audit — certificate pinning, encrypted local storage, jailbreak/root detection, and OWASP Mobile Top 10 checks — before submission.

05

Launch & ASO

We handle full App Store and Google Play submission with Arabic and English metadata, UAE region ASO keyword research, and a launch plan designed to drive organic installs from Abu Dhabi users from day one.
