Overview

What is a UAE Employment Visa in Abu Dhabi?

A legal requirement for every foreign national working under an Abu Dhabi-based company — no exceptions under UAE labour law.

A UAE employment visa is a government-issued residency permit that authorises a foreign national to legally live and work in Abu Dhabi under employer sponsorship. Under UAE labour law, every foreign national employed by an Abu Dhabi-based company must hold a valid employment visa and MOHRE work permit.

In Abu Dhabi, the employer is the visa sponsor and bears full legal and financial responsibility for the employee's residency status — including initiating the visa, maintaining compliance, and cancelling the visa when employment ends.

The Abu Dhabi employment visa involves five government authorities: MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation), GDRFA / ICA (Federal Authority for Identity and Citizenship), and EIDA (Emirates Identity Authority). Each has its own portal, documentation requirements, and processing timeline. TamkinExpress manages every interaction with every authority on your behalf.

Inside Country vs Outside Country — Abu Dhabi Visa Change

A common question from Abu Dhabi employers: does the employee need to leave the UAE during the visa process?

Inside country processing — also called a change of status — means the employee's visa is changed without leaving the UAE. Faster, less disruptive, and avoids travel expenses. Eligibility depends on the employee's current visa type. TamkinExpress confirms eligibility before initiating.

Outside country processing requires the employee to exit the UAE — typically to Oman or their home country — and re-enter on the newly issued employment entry permit. Always available for all nationalities. The entry permit is valid for 60 days from issuance.

TamkinExpress assesses each employee's current visa status and recommends the most efficient and cost-effective route for your Abu Dhabi business.

Employment Visa Violations & Penalties in Abu Dhabi

UAE immigration and MOHRE authorities actively monitor employer compliance. Violations directly impact your ability to hire and operate.

⚠️
Overstay Penalty
AED 25 Per Day
Employees with expired visas accumulate daily overstay fines from the date of expiry. The employer is jointly responsible — fines must be settled before any new visa is processed.
🚫
Cancellation Failure
Quota Block
Failing to cancel visas for departed employees blocks the quota slot — preventing new hires until the cancellation is completed and the slot is released.
💰
Illegal Employment
AED 50,000+ Fine
Employing a worker without a valid MOHRE work permit or on a cancelled visa is a serious violation with significant financial penalties under UAE labour law.
🛑
Repeated Violations
MOHRE Permit Freeze
Repeated compliance violations result in MOHRE freezing all new work permit applications for the company — a full hiring ban until all violations are resolved.
