A UAE employment visa authorises a foreign national to legally live and work in Abu Dhabi under employer sponsorship. Under UAE labour law, every foreign national employed by an Abu Dhabi company must hold a valid employment visa and MOHRE work permit — no exceptions, whether the company is an ADDED-licensed mainland business or a free zone entity.

In Abu Dhabi, the employer is the visa sponsor and bears full legal and financial responsibility for the employee's residency status throughout employment — including initiating the visa, paying overstay fines if they occur, and cancelling the visa when employment ends.

The process involves three government authorities: MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation) for work permit, GDRFA / ICA (Federal Authority for Identity and Citizenship) for entry permit and residency stamping, and EIDA (Emirates Identity Authority) for Emirates ID. Processing Options

Inside Country vs Outside Country —
Abu Dhabi Visa Change

A common question from Abu Dhabi employers: does the employee need to leave the UAE during the visa process?

Inside country processing — also called a change of status — means the employee's visa is changed without leaving the UAE. Faster, less disruptive, and avoids travel costs. Eligibility depends on the employee's current visa type and status. TamkinExpress confirms eligibility before initiating.

Outside country processing requires the employee to exit the UAE — typically to Oman or their home country — and re-enter on the employment entry permit. Always available for all nationalities. The entry permit is valid for 60 days from issuance. Standard process for new hires joining from abroad.
