Overview

What is a UAE Employment Visa?

A UAE employment visa is a government-issued residency permit that allows a foreign national to legally live and work in the UAE under employer sponsorship.

In Abu Dhabi, the employer is the sponsor and is legally responsible for initiating, managing, and — when necessary — cancelling the employment visa. The employment visa is not a single document — it is a series of approvals issued by multiple government authorities: MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation), GDRFA / ICA (General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs), and EIDA (Emirates Identity Authority). Each authority has its own portal, documentation requirements, and processing timeline that must be managed in sequence.

Processing Options

Inside Country vs Outside Country — Abu Dhabi Visa Change

One of the most common questions Abu Dhabi employers ask is whether the employee needs to leave the country or can stay inside the UAE.

Inside country processing — also called a change of status — means the employee's visa is changed without leaving the UAE. Faster, less disruptive, and avoids travel expenses. Not always available depending on the employee's current visa type.

Outside country processing requires the employee to exit the UAE — typically to Oman or their home country — and re-enter on the newly issued employment entry permit. Always available and the standard process for employees hired from outside the UAE. The entry permit is valid for 60 days from issuance.

TamkinExpress assesses each employee's current status and advises on the most efficient route — saving time and avoiding unnecessary costs for your Abu Dhabi business.

Employment Visa Violations & Penalties in Abu Dhabi

UAE immigration and labour authorities actively monitor employer compliance. Violations directly impact your ability to hire new staff and operate your business.

⚠️
Overstay Penalty
AED 25 Per Day
Employees with expired visas in Abu Dhabi accumulate daily overstay fines. The employer is jointly responsible for payment before a new visa can be issued.
🚫
Cancellation Failure
Hiring Freeze Risk
Failing to cancel visas for departed employees blocks the quota slot — preventing the employer from hiring a replacement.
💰
Illegal Employment
AED 50,000+ Fine
Employing a worker in Abu Dhabi without a valid work permit or on a cancelled visa is a serious violation with significant financial penalties.
🛑
Repeated Violations
MOHRE Permit Freeze
Repeated violations result in MOHRE freezing all new work permit applications for the company until violations are fully resolved.
