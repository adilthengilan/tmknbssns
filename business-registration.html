Why UAE

Why Register a Company in UAE?
Top Reasons for UAE Business Setup

The UAE is a business-friendly destination for company formation, with no personal income tax, 100% foreign ownership for most activities, and strong infrastructure in Abu Dhabi and Dubai.

🏆

100% Foreign Ownership

Since the 2021 company law reforms, foreigners can own 100% of a mainland company for most business activities, with no local sponsor required for most.
💰

No Personal Income Tax

The UAE levies no personal income tax. Qualifying free zone companies can enjoy 0% corporate tax on qualifying income, while standard UAE corporate tax rules apply to other income.
🌍

Strategic Global Hub

Abu Dhabi and Dubai offer access to GCC, African, Asian and European markets, which suits international trade and investment.
🏃

Fast Company Registration

Company formation can take as little as a few days for some free zones and around 7 working days for mainland, depending on the activity and authority.
🏛️

Stable Business Environment

Strong legal frameworks, investor-friendly policies and a transparent regulatory environment support long-term business planning.
🪪

Investor Visa & UAE Residency

Company owners can apply for an investor visa and UAE residence. See our visa services and the Golden Visa for long-term residency options.
