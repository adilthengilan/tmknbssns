Frequently Asked Questions

How long does business registration take in UAE?

Typically 5-10 working days for mainland companies and 3-5 days for free zone companies, depending on the business activity and documentation completeness.

What are the minimum capital requirements?

Minimum share capital varies by emirate and business type. For LLC in Dubai mainland, it's AED 300,000, while free zones may have lower requirements.

Can foreigners own 100% of a business in UAE?

Yes, foreigners can own 100% of companies in UAE free zones and mainland companies under the new UAE Commercial Companies Law.

What documents are required for business registration?

Passport copies, visa copies, NOC from sponsor (if applicable), business plan, and specific documents based on business activity.

Do I need a local sponsor for my business?

Not required for free zone companies or certain mainland activities under the new Commercial Companies Law. We'll advise based on your business type.

What ongoing compliance is required?

Annual license renewal, maintaining corporate bank accounts, filing annual returns, and compliance with UAE labor and tax laws.
