1. What are PRO Services in UAE?

PRO stands for Public Relations Officer. A PRO acts as the official representative of a company when dealing with UAE government authorities — submitting documents, obtaining approvals, and managing all government-related procedures.

Outsourcing PRO services to a specialist company like Tamkin Express means you get expert handling of all government procedures without needing a dedicated in-house team.

5. PRO Services vs GRO Services

PRO (Public Relations Officer) handles day-to-day government documentation — visas, labour cards, licence renewals. These are routine, recurring procedures.

GRO (Government Relations Officer) manages higher-level government relations — ministry approvals, regulatory compliance, government contracts, and strategic government liaison. Tamkin Express provides both PRO and GRO services.
