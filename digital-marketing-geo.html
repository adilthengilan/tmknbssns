What AI Systems Look For

To be cited by AI systems, your brand needs to build signals that language models use to assess credibility, relevance, and geographic authority.

  • E-E-A-T signals: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness
  • Consistent brand entity across Wikipedia, Wikidata, Google Knowledge Graph
  • Authoritative third-party mentions and citations in trusted sources
  • Comprehensive structured data (Schema.org markup) across your site
  • Location-specific content that clearly signals emirate and district coverage
  • Arabic content that serves Gulf AI query patterns natively
  • FAQ content that directly matches how buyers phrase AI questions
