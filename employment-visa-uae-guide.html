Employment Visa UAE 2025 — Step by Step Process Guide

By Tamkin Express 📅 March 2026 ⏱ 7 min read

Getting an employment visa in UAE is one of the most common procedures for companies hiring staff and employees relocating to the UAE. This guide covers the complete employment visa process in UAE — documents required, processing time, costs, and how Tamkin Express can handle it for you.

💡 Quick Summary: Employment visa processing takes 5–10 working days. Cost ranges from AED 1,400–1,800. Both MOHRE work permit and ICA residence visa are required. Tamkin Express handles the complete process.

1. What is an Employment Visa in UAE?

An employment visa (also called a residence visa) allows a foreign national to legally live and work in UAE. It is linked to an employer and consists of two parts:

  • Work Permit — issued by MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources & Emiratisation) — allows legal work
  • Residence Visa — issued by ICA (Federal Authority for Identity & Citizenship) — allows legal residence

Both are required for every employee working in UAE mainland companies.

2. Employment Visa Process — Step by Step

Step 1: Work Permit Approval (MOHRE)

The employer submits a work permit application through the MOHRE portal. This requires the employee's passport copy, educational certificates, and a signed labour contract.

Step 2: Entry Permit

Once the work permit is approved, an entry permit is issued — allowing the employee to enter UAE for the purpose of employment.

Step 3: Status Change (if already in UAE)

If the employee is already in UAE on a visit or tourist visa, a status change application is filed to convert their status to residence visa.

Step 4: Medical Fitness Test

All employees must undergo a medical fitness test at an approved UAE health authority centre. This screens for communicable diseases.

Step 5: Emirates ID Registration

Biometric registration and Emirates ID application are submitted through the Federal Authority for Identity and Citizenship.

Step 6: Residence Visa Stamping

The final residence visa is stamped in the employee's passport — completing the employment visa process.

⏱ Total timeline: 5–10 working days for standard processing. 48 hours for express processing through Tamkin Express.

3. Documents Required for Employment Visa UAE

  • Original passport (valid for minimum 6 months)
  • Passport-size photograph (white background)
  • Educational certificates (attested)
  • Signed labour contract
  • Employer's valid trade licence copy
  • Medical fitness test results
  • Emirates ID (for status change)

5. Employment Visa Renewal in UAE

Employment visas in UAE are typically valid for 2 years and must be renewed before expiry to avoid fines. Renewal requires similar documents plus Emirates ID renewal and updated medical test.

Tamkin Express proactively tracks expiry dates and handles renewals to ensure zero overstay risk for your workforce.

6. Family Visa Sponsorship UAE

Employment visa holders can sponsor their spouse, children, and parents for UAE residence. Requirements include minimum salary threshold (usually AED 4,000–5,000/month depending on emirate) and suitable accommodation proof.
