Understanding Employment Visa Sharjah

What is a UAE Employment Visa
and How Does It Work in Sharjah?

A UAE employment visa is a government-issued residency permit allowing a foreign national to legally live and work in the UAE under employer sponsorship. In Sharjah, the employer is the legal sponsor and bears full legal and financial responsibility for initiating, managing, and cancelling the employment visa throughout the employee’s tenure.

The employment visa is a series of approvals across multiple government authorities — MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation), GDRFA / ICA (Federal Authority for Identity and Citizenship), and EIDA (Emirates Identity Authority). Each has its own portal, documentation requirements, and processing timeline. For Sharjah businesses, this means navigating SEDD licensing requirements alongside MOHRE and GDRFA Sharjah — a combination that requires experience with Sharjah-specific authority portals and documentation formats.

This is why Sharjah businesses rely on professional employment visa service providers like TamkinExpress — to ensure every stage is handled correctly, on time, and in full compliance with UAE immigration and labour law, without your HR team navigating multiple government portals.

Processing Options

Inside Country vs Outside Country
Sharjah Employment Visa Processing

One of the most common questions Sharjah employers ask is whether the employee must exit the UAE to process their employment visa. Both routes are available, and TamkinExpress advises on the most suitable option for each case.

Inside country processing — also called a change of status — converts the employee’s current visa to the new Sharjah employment visa without leaving the UAE. Faster and less disruptive. TamkinExpress confirms eligibility before proceeding — availability depends on the employee’s current UAE visa status.

Outside country processing requires the employee to exit the UAE and re-enter on the employment entry permit. Always available for all nationalities. The entry permit is valid for 60 days from issuance. TamkinExpress coordinates timing to avoid expiry risk.

TamkinExpress assesses each employee’s current status and advises on the most efficient route for your Sharjah business — saving time and avoiding unnecessary costs.

Inside Country — Change of StatusNo travel required. Employee remains in UAE. Faster and less disruptive — ideal when already in UAE on a valid visa.
Outside Country — Exit & Re-EntryEmployee exits UAE and re-enters on the employment entry permit. Always available — standard for new hires coming from abroad.
