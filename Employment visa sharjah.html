Employment Visa Services in Sharjah
— What You Need to Know

Sharjah is the third-largest emirate in the UAE and home to a significant industrial, manufacturing, and trading business community. The emirate hosts several major free zones — including SAIF Zone (Sharjah Airport International Free Zone) and Hamriyah Free Zone — alongside a large mainland business sector registered with SEDD (Sharjah Economic Development Department).

Employment visa processing in Sharjah follows the federal MOHRE framework for work permits and the GDRFA Sharjah for residence visa stamping. Free zone companies in SAIF Zone and Hamriyah Free Zone follow their respective authority procedures before the GDRFA residence visa stage. TamkinExpress navigates all of these pathways on behalf of Sharjah employers.

With Sharjah's proximity to Dubai, many companies have offices or operations in both emirates. TamkinExpress provides seamless employment visa management for businesses operating across both Sharjah and Dubai — a common requirement for logistics, construction, and trading companies.
