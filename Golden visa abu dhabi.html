What Is the UAE Golden Visa?

Abu Dhabi is the UAE's federal capital — home to ADNOC, ADGM (Abu Dhabi Global Market), Masdar City, Cleveland Clinic, sovereign wealth funds, and a large concentration of government and semi-government employers.
