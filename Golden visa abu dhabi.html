UAE Golden Visa in Abu Dhabi — What You Need to Know

A 10-year, self-sponsored residency that gives you full independence in the UAE — no employer required.

The UAE Golden Visa is a 10-year, self-sponsored residency that allows investors, professionals, entrepreneurs, and students to live and work in the UAE independently — no employer sponsor required. In Abu Dhabi, Golden Visa applications are processed through ICA (Identity and Citizenship Authority) with coordination through ADDED for mainland investors and ADGM for free zone profiles.
