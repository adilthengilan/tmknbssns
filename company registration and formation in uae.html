Required Documents

Documents Needed for
Company Registration UAE

We provide a complete checklist after your free consultation. Here's what most registrations require:

🪪

Passport Copies

Colour copies of passports for all shareholders and managers involved in the company.

🆔

Emirates ID (Residents)

Required for UAE residents who are shareholders or managers of the company.

📝

Trade Name Proposal

3–5 proposed company names in order of preference for authority approval.

🏬

Office Tenancy / Flexi-Desk

Ejari tenancy contract or flexi-desk agreement as proof of registered address.

📋

Business Activity Description

A clear description of all business activities to be licensed under the company.

🖊️

MOA / SHA (if applicable)

Memorandum of Association and Shareholder Agreement for multi-partner setups.
