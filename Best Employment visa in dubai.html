What Is a UAE Employment Visa in Dubai?

Legal requirements every Dubai employer must understand

A UAE employment visa is a government-issued residency permit that authorises a foreign national to legally live and work in the UAE under employer sponsorship. In Dubai, the employer is the legal visa sponsor — responsible for initiating, maintaining, and cancelling the visa when employment ends.

Under UAE labour law, every foreign national employed by a Dubai-based company must hold a valid employment visa and work permit. There are no exceptions. This applies to all mainland companies (licensed by DET) and all free zone companies (DMCC, JAFZA, DIFC, DIC, and others).

The employment visa process in Dubai involves five government authorities: MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources), GDRFA (General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs), and ICA / EIDA (Federal Authority for Identity and Citizenship). Employment Entry Permit — GDRFA

Entry permit issued by GDRFA — allowing the employee to enter the UAE legally for visa stamping. Valid for 60 days from issue date. Typical timeline: 2–5 working days.

Residency Visa Stamping

A 2-year UAE residency visa is stamped in the passport and digitally linked to the Emirates ID. Your Dubai employee is fully legal and ready to begin work. Typical timeline: 3–7 working days.

How long does employment visa processing take in Dubai?
The complete Dubai employment visa process — from work permit application to residency visa stamping — typically takes 2 to 4 weeks when all documents are correctly submitted. The five stages break down as: MOHRE work permit (3–5 days), GDRFA entry permit (2–5 days), medical fitness test (1–3 days), Emirates ID biometrics (2–7 days), and residency visa stamping (3–7 days). TamkinExpress manages every stage with daily follow-up to ensure the fastest possible processing.
What documents are required for a UAE employment visa in Dubai?
Employer documents required: Valid Dubai trade licence, establishment card (Tawjeeh card), and signed employment contract. Employee documents required: Valid passport with at least 6 months remaining validity, passport-size photograph, and educational certificates for professional visa categories. TamkinExpress provides a complete, role-specific document checklist after a free consultation, confirming all requirements before submission.
What is the difference between inside country and outside country employment visa processing?
Inside country processing allows the employee to remain in the UAE throughout the entire visa process — faster and less disruptive for employees already in Dubai on a valid tourist, visit, or other employer visa. Not all visa statuses qualify for inside country change of status. Outside country processing requires the employee to exit the UAE and re-enter on the employment entry permit — always available for all nationalities and all visa categories. TamkinExpress assesses each employee's current visa status and recommends the most suitable and cost-effective route before initiating.
What are the fines for expired employment visas in Dubai?
An expired employment visa in Dubai results in AED 25 per day in overstay fines, accumulating from the day of expiry until the visa is renewed, transferred, or the employee exits the UAE. The employer is jointly liable for all overstay fines — they must be settled before a new or renewed visa can be processed. Employing a worker without a valid work permit carries financial penalties exceeding AED 50,000 under UAE labour law. TamkinExpress maintains a proactive compliance calendar for every client to ensure no visa expires without advance action.

Who is legally responsible for an employment visa in Dubai?
In Dubai, the employer is the visa sponsor and bears full legal and financial responsibility for the employee's residency status throughout their employment. This includes initiating the visa, maintaining compliance, paying any overstay fines, and — when employment ends — cancelling the visa within the required timeframe. Failure to cancel visas for departed employees blocks quota slots and can trigger compliance penalties from MOHRE.
What is the validity of a UAE employment visa in Dubai?
A standard UAE employment visa in Dubai is valid for 2 years and is digitally linked to the Emirates ID. The employment entry permit (issued before the employee enters the UAE) is valid for 60 days from the date of issue — the employee must complete the visa stamping process within this window.
