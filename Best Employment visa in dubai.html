Overview

What is a UAE Employment Visa in Dubai?

If you are hiring employees for your Dubai business, an employment visa is not optional — it is a legal requirement for every foreign national working in the UAE.

A UAE employment visa is a government-issued residency permit that allows a foreign national to legally live and work in the UAE under employer sponsorship. In Dubai, the employer is the visa sponsor and is legally responsible for initiating, managing, and — when employment ends — cancelling the employment visa. The employer bears legal and financial responsibility for the employee's residency status throughout their time in the UAE.

Processing Options

Inside Country vs Outside Country Dubai Employment Visa

One of the most common questions Dubai employers ask is whether the employee needs to leave the UAE. TamkinExpress advises on the most suitable option for each specific case.

Inside Country

Change of Status — No Travel Required

  • Employee remains in UAE throughout the process
  • Faster and less disruptive for existing Dubai residents
  • Available for employees on valid tourist, visit, or other employer visas
  • TamkinExpress confirms eligibility before initiating — not all statuses qualify
  • Ideal when the employee is already working in Dubai
Outside Country

Exit & Re-Entry — Always Available

  • Employee exits UAE and re-enters on the employment entry permit
  • Standard route for new hires joining from outside UAE
  • Entry permit valid 60 days — TamkinExpress coordinates timing to prevent expiry
  • Available for all nationalities and all visa categories
  • Required when inside country change is not eligible
Compliance Risk

Employment Visa Violations & Penalties in Dubai

UAE immigration authorities actively monitor employer compliance in Dubai. Violations result in penalties that directly block your ability to hire and operate.

Overstay Fine
AED 25 / Day

Every employee with an expired Dubai residence visa accumulates daily overstay fines. The employer is jointly responsible for payment before a new visa can be processed.

Uncancelled Visa
Quota Block

Failing to cancel visas for departed Dubai employees blocks the quota slot, preventing the employer from hiring a replacement until the cancellation is processed.

Illegal Employment
AED 50,000+ Fine

Employing a worker in Dubai without a valid work permit or on a cancelled visa is a serious violation with significant financial penalties under UAE labour law.

MOHRE Permit Freeze
Hiring Ban

Repeated compliance violations in Dubai result in MOHRE freezing all new work permit applications for the company — preventing any new staff additions.
