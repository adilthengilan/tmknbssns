The UAE Golden Visa is a 10-year, self-sponsored residency permit with no employer requirement and no travel restrictions. It was introduced by the UAE federal government to attract investors, entrepreneurs, professionals, and students. In Musaffah, qualifying categories include property investors with AED 2M+ in UAE real estate, entrepreneurs with UAE-registered businesses meeting ICA criteria, skilled professionals meeting salary thresholds with a nomination letter, and outstanding university graduates.

UAE Golden Visa processing for Musaffah residents typically takes 30 days from the date all required documents are submitted. The timeline covers ICA application submission, ICA review and approval, medical fitness test coordination, Emirates ID biometric registration with EIDA, and final visa issuance.

TamkinExpress follows up with ICA daily on behalf of Musaffah clients, ensuring no delays caused by pending queries. Yes. All UAE Golden Visa holders including those based in Musaffah can sponsor their immediate family for 10-year UAE residency. Eligible family members include the spouse, children of any age (including adults over 18, which is not possible under standard UAE residency), and both parents of the primary visa holder.

Family sponsorship requires its own ICA application with documents including the family member's passport, photo, health insurance, and family relationship certificate. TamkinExpress handles complete family sponsorship either simultaneously with the primary Golden Visa or separately if the primary visa has already been issued.

This makes the Golden Visa particularly valuable for business owners with international operations and families with members studying or working abroad. The 10-year visa is renewable upon expiry provided the qualifying criterion is maintained.

It is submitted to ICA as part of the application documentation and is one of the most commonly missing documents in professional Golden Visa applications, causing avoidable delays.

TamkinExpress guides Musaffah employers on the exact content, format, and signatory requirements for a valid nomination letter — preventing delays that occur when letters are incorrectly issued.
