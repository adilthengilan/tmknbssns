What Is the UAE Golden Visa?

The UAE Golden Visa is a 10-year, self-sponsored residency with no employer requirement and no travel restrictions. It allows investors, entrepreneurs, skilled professionals, and students to live and work in the UAE independently. Family members — spouse, children of any age including adults, and both parents — can be sponsored. Unlike standard UAE employer-sponsored residency, the Golden Visa never expires due to travel and requires no renewal of employer relationship.
