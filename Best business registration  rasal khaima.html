Business Registration in Ras Al Khaimah — What It Means

Ras Al Khaimah is a key commercial and industrial hub in the UAE. Every business operating here requires a valid RAK DED / RAKEZ trade license — and TamkinExpress navigates that process for you.

What Is a RAK Trade License?

Ras Al Khaimah businesses may register either through the RAK Department of Economic Development (mainland) or the Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone (RAKEZ) — one of the UAE's largest and most cost-effective free zones. A valid license from either authority is mandatory for legal business operations, bank account opening, and employee sponsorship. RAKEZ combines multiple former free zones into a single, streamlined authority.

RAKEZ (Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone) is a consolidated free zone authority combining Al Hamra, RAK, and Ras Al Khaimah Free Trade Zones. It offers 100% foreign ownership, no corporate or personal income tax, competitive license fees, dedicated industrial facilities, and a streamlined registration process — making it one of the UAE's most attractive investment destinations.
RAKEZ hosts a diverse mix of industries: ceramics and stone products (RAK is the world's leading ceramics exporter), pharmaceuticals, steel fabrication, plastics, food processing, paper, and chemical manufacturing.
