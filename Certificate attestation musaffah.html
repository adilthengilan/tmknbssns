What is the difference between attestation and apostille in Musaffah?

Attestation vs Apostille — Musaffah Explained

Attestation is required for documents submitted to UAE government authorities, courts, and embassies — it involves stamps from MOFA and other UAE bodies (MOHRE, MOE). Apostille is an international certification under the Hague Convention for documents used in Hague-signatory countries. TamkinExpress in Musaffah handles both — advise on destination country and document purpose to confirm which process applies.
