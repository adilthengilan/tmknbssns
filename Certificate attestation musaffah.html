What is the difference between attestation and apostille in Musaffah?
Attestation is used for documents going to non-Hague Convention countries and requires processing through the home country embassy and UAE MOFA. Apostille (single stamp certification) is used for Hague Convention member countries. As of 2024, the UAE joined the Hague Convention, making apostille available for certain international document verifications. TamkinExpress handles both processes for Musaffah clients.
