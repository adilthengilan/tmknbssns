What Is Custom Software?

Custom software is a software application built from scratch to match the exact workflows, processes, and requirements of a specific business. Unlike off-the-shelf software (such as standard accounting tools or generic CRM platforms), custom software does exactly what the Dubai business needs — no compromises, no forcing processes to fit a product's limitations. Examples include a bespoke ERP for a Dubai trading company, a patient management system for a DIFC medical clinic, or a fleet tracking dashboard for a logistics company in Jebel Ali.

Step 3: Agile Development

Step 6: Post-Launch Support & Maintenance
