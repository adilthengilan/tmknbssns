What Is a Mobile App?

A mobile application is a software program designed and built to run natively on smartphones and tablets — available for download from the Apple App Store (iOS) and Google Play Store (Android). Unlike mobile websites, a native or cross-platform mobile app delivers a faster, more engaging experience — with offline functionality, push notifications, device camera and GPS access, biometric login, and platform-specific UI patterns. For UAQ businesses, a mobile app puts your service directly on your customers' phones — accessible 24/7, without a browser.
