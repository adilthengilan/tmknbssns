Frequently Asked Questions

How long does employment visa processing take?

Typically 5-10 working days for standard processing. Express processing is available for urgent cases.

What salary is required to sponsor a family visa?

A minimum salary of AED 4,000 (or AED 3,000 + accommodation) is generally required to sponsor family members.

Can I sponsor my parents on a UAE visa?

Yes, parents can be sponsored under specific conditions. We can guide you through the eligibility criteria and process.

What documents are needed for a family visa?

Passport copies, marriage certificate, birth certificates (for children), salary certificate, tenancy contract, and sponsor's visa copy.

Can I change jobs on an employment visa?

Yes, you can transfer your visa to a new employer. We handle all the paperwork for visa transfer and cancellation.

What happens if my visa expires?

Overstaying a visa results in fines. Contact us immediately for visa renewal or status regularization before expiry.
