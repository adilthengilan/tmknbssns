Understanding Employment Visa UAE

What is a UAE Employment Visa —
and How Does It Work?

A UAE employment visa is a government-issued residency permit that allows a foreign national to legally live and work in the UAE under employer sponsorship. It is not a single document — it is a series of approvals issued by multiple government authorities including MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation), GDRFA (General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs), and EIDA (Emirates Identity Authority).

The employer is the sponsor and is responsible for initiating, managing, and — when necessary — cancelling the employment visa. This means the employer bears the legal and financial responsibility for the employee's residency status throughout their time in the UAE. Errors in the process, missed renewals, or improper cancellations create liability for the sponsoring company.

This is precisely why companies across Abu Dhabi and Dubai rely on professional employment visa service providers like TamkinExpress — to ensure every stage of the visa lifecycle is handled correctly, on time, and in full compliance with UAE immigration and labour law.

Processing Options

Inside Country vs Outside Country —
UAE Employment Visa Change

One of the most common questions employers ask about employment visa processing in UAE is whether the employee needs to leave the country or whether the visa can be processed while they remain inside the UAE. Both routes are available, and TamkinExpress advises on the most suitable option for each case.

Inside country processing — also called a change of status — means the employee's visa is changed from their current status (tourist visa, visit visa, or another employer's residency) to the new employment visa without leaving the UAE. This is faster, less disruptive, and avoids travel expenses. However, it is not always available depending on the employee's current visa type and status.

Outside country processing requires the employee to exit the UAE — typically to Oman or their home country — and re-enter on the newly issued employment entry permit. This route is always available and is the standard process for employees hired from outside the UAE. The entry permit is valid for 60 days from issuance, and the employee must enter before it expires.

TamkinExpress assesses each employee's current status and advises on the most efficient route — saving employers time and avoiding the additional costs associated with unnecessary travel.

Employment Visa
Violations & Penalties

UAE immigration and labour authorities actively monitor employer compliance. Companies that allow employment visas to expire, fail to cancel visas when employees leave, or process visas through incorrect channels face penalties that directly impact their ability to hire new staff and operate their business.

The most common employment visa violations in the UAE include expired residency visas, failure to cancel visas for employees who have left the company, employing workers on visit visas or cancelled visas, and using unauthorised channels for visa processing.

TamkinExpress maintains a compliance calendar for every client — tracking visa expiry dates, entry permit windows, and renewal deadlines — and initiates action before violations occur, not after.
