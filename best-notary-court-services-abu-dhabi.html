Notarization is performed by a UAE Notary Public and verifies the authenticity of a document's content and the identity of the signing parties — required for documents used within the UAE for legal transactions such as property sales, Power of Attorney, and business agreements.

Attestation is an additional process where a government authority (Ministry of Justice, MOFA, or an embassy) officially endorses the notarized document for recognition in another country. In simple terms: notarization is for local UAE legal use, attestation is for international recognition. TamkinExpress provides both services in Abu Dhabi.
