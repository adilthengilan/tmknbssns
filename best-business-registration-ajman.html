Business Registration in Ajman — What It Means

Ajman is a key commercial and industrial hub in the UAE. Every business operating here requires a valid Ajman DED trade license — and TamkinExpress navigates that process for you.

What Is an Ajman DED License?

The Ajman Department of Economic Development (Ajman DED) is the sole authority licensing all commercial, industrial, professional, and workshop activities on the Ajman mainland. Every legal business in Ajman requires a valid DED trade license — from a small retail shop to a large LLC. Without it, your company cannot open a bank account, sponsor employees, or legally operate.
