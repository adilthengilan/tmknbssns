Understanding Attestation

How Does Document Attestation
Work in the UAE?

Certificate attestation is the official process of confirming that a document is genuine, so UAE authorities and institutions will accept it. It usually involves a chain of stamps from the issuing authority, then the ministry that verifies that type of document, and finally the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) or the relevant embassy.

The exact chain depends on where the document was issued and what it is. Educational, personal and commercial documents each follow a different path, and documents from other countries follow the home country's process before UAE attestation. Depending on the issuing country, an apostille may replace embassy legalisation in some cases, so we confirm the correct route before starting.

Skipping a step or using the wrong sequence is the most common reason attestation is delayed or rejected. Tamkin Express reviews your documents first, then handles each stage and keeps you updated until delivery.

Document TypeTypical Route (UAE-Issued)Commonly Used For
Educational certificatesMinistry of Education → MOFAEmployment visa, further study, licensing
Personal documentsMinistry of Justice / relevant authority → MOFAFamily visa, legal and civil use
Commercial documentsChamber of Commerce / relevant authority → MOFACompany setup, trade, banking
Documents from other countriesHome-country authority → UAE embassy (or apostille where applicable) → MOFAUse in the UAE

Routes shown are indicative and can vary by emirate, issuing country and document. Contact us and we will confirm the exact path for your case.
