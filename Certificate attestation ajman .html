Certificate Attestation in Ajman — Explained

Definition

Certificate attestation in the UAE is the official process of authenticating documents through UAE government authorities — including the Ministry of Education, Ministry of Justice, and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) — so they are legally recognized by UAE employers, visa authorities, courts, and international institutions. A document is only considered legally valid in the UAE once it carries the official government attestation stamp.

What is the difference between MOFA attestation and embassy attestation in Ajman?
