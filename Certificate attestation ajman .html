What is certificate attestation and why is it required in Ajman?
Certificate attestation is the official process of authenticating documents through UAE government authorities — Ministry of Education, Ministry of Justice, and MOFA — so they are recognized in Ajman. It is required for employment visa applications, family visa sponsorship, Ajman Free Zone professional licensing, and business setup. Without proper attestation, documents are not accepted by MOHRE, Ajman courts, or government departments.

What is the difference between MOFA attestation and embassy attestation in Ajman?
MOFA attestation is the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs' official authentication — required for documents to be recognized within the UAE or as the final UAE step internationally. Embassy attestation is performed by a country's embassy in the UAE — required for documents intended for use in a specific foreign country.
