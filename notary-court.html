Understanding Notarization

What is Document Notarization
in the UAE?

Document notarization in the UAE is the official process of having a document, signature or legal act verified by a licensed notary public authority so that it carries legal weight. Notarized documents are accepted by courts, government departments, banks and employers, which makes notarization a key step in property deals, company changes, family matters and employment procedures.

Mistakes at this stage are costly. A document with a wrong name spelling, a missing translation or an unsuitable format can be returned by the authority, delaying a court hearing, a company amendment or a visa application. That is why individuals and companies in Abu Dhabi and Dubai work with experienced notary and legal documentation specialists like Tamkin Express.

Notarization vs
Attestation

Notarization authenticates a document through a UAE notary public for local legal use. Attestation is the next layer: a ministry or embassy officially recognises the document, usually so it can be used internationally or in a government procedure.

Many documents need both, in the right order. Skipping a step or doing it in the wrong sequence is the most common reason applications are sent back.
