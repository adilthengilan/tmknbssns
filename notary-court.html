Everything you need to know about notary and court services in UAE

Document notarization is the official process by which a UAE Notary Public authenticates and certifies a document, confirming its validity for legal use. It is required for power of attorney documents, commercial contracts, real estate transactions, court submissions, and any document that must be presented to a UAE government authority or used internationally. Without notarization, such documents are not legally recognized.

A Power of Attorney (POA) is a legal document authorizing a person or entity to act on your behalf for specific or general matters — such as signing contracts, managing property, or handling government procedures. In the UAE, a POA must be drafted correctly and notarized by the UAE Notary Public to be legally valid. TamkinExpress handles the full process: drafting, translation if required, and official notarization.

Most standard document notarization is completed within 24–48 hours. Processing time may vary based on the document type, the authority involved, and whether translation is required. For urgent matters, we offer priority processing. Contact us to confirm the timeline for your specific document.

Notarization is performed by a UAE Notary Public and confirms the authenticity of a document's signature or content. Ministry of Justice (MOJ) attestation is an additional layer of official verification that confirms a notarized document is valid under UAE law and is often required for documents used in court proceedings, government submissions, or international use. TamkinExpress manages both processes on your behalf.
