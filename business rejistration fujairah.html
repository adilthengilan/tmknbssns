Business Registration in Fujairah — What It Means

Fujairah is a key commercial and industrial hub in the UAE. Every business operating here requires a valid Fujairah ED trade license — and TamkinExpress navigates that process for you.

What Is a Fujairah Economic Department License?

The Fujairah Economic Department (FED) is the licensing authority for all commercial, industrial, professional, and contracting activities on the Fujairah mainland. A valid FED trade license is required for any legal business operation in Fujairah — from a trading company to a manufacturing plant. It is the foundation for bank account opening, employee sponsorship, and legal business activity.
