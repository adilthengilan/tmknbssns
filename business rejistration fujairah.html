Overview

What is Business Registration in Fujairah?

Fujairah's Gulf of Oman coastline makes it a strategic hub for maritime, oil & gas, logistics, and creative industries.

Business registration in Fujairah means obtaining a trade license from the Fujairah Department of Economic Development (FDED) for mainland companies, or from Fujairah Freezone (FFZ) or Creative City Fujairah for freezone setups. Fujairah's unique location on the Gulf of Oman makes it ideal for shipping, storage, oil & gas, and logistics businesses. Creative City Fujairah is the top choice for freelancers and media professionals. TamkinExpress manages every step — name reservation, FDED approvals, MOA notarization, and visa processing.
