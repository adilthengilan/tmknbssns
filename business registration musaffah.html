What Is an ADDED Trade License?

The Abu Dhabi Department of Economic Development (ADDED) is the sole licensing authority for all commercial, industrial, professional, and workshop activities in Musaffah. A valid ADDED trade license is mandatory for any legal business operation — from a single-owner workshop to a multi-shareholder LLC. Without it, your business cannot open a bank account, hire employees, or operate on the premises.
