What Is an ADDED Trade License?

The Abu Dhabi Department of Economic Development (ADDED) is the sole licensing authority for all commercial, industrial, professional, and workshop activities in Musaffah. A valid ADDED trade license is mandatory for any legal business operation — from a single-owner workshop to a multi-shareholder LLC. Without a trade license, a business cannot open a corporate bank account, hire employees under an establishment card, or legally operate on Musaffah premises.
