Overview

What Are Notary & Court Services?

In the UAE, notarized documents are legally required for countless personal, business, and legal transactions.

Notary and court services in Fujairah cover the official attestation, authentication, and legal certification of documents through the UAE Ministry of Justice and Fujairah Court. This includes Power of Attorney (POA), affidavits, tenancy agreements, sale contracts, company documents, and personal status declarations. Without proper notarization, these documents are not legally recognized in the UAE or abroad.
