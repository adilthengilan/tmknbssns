Frequently Asked Questions

What is the UAE Golden Visa?

The UAE Golden Visa is a long-term residency visa valid for 5 or 10 years, granted to investors, entrepreneurs, skilled professionals, and outstanding students.

How much investment is needed for a Golden Visa?

Real estate investment of AED 2 million or more qualifies for a Golden Visa. Other categories have different requirements based on the visa type.

Can my family get a Golden Visa too?

Yes, Golden Visa holders can sponsor their spouse, children of all ages, and in some cases, parents and domestic workers.

Do I need to stay in UAE to keep my Golden Visa?

Unlike regular UAE visas, Golden Visa holders are not required to return to UAE every 6 months to maintain their visa status.

Can employees apply for a Golden Visa?

Yes, skilled professionals with a monthly salary of AED 30,000 or more in specific professions can apply for a Golden Visa.

How long does the Golden Visa process take?

Typically 2-4 weeks from document submission to visa issuance, depending on the category and document readiness.
