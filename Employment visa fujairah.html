FOIZ companies require specialist MOHRE work permit categories for maritime, oil field, and energy sector employees — categories that differ significantly from standard commercial work permits. How to Get an Employment Visa
in Fujairah — 5 Steps

TamkinExpress handles all 5 stages of the Fujairah employment visa — from MOHRE work permit to GDRFA Fujairah residency stamping. You only need to provide documents.

1

Step 1: Document Review & Collection

Provide employer trade licence (Fujairah DED or FFZ/FOIZ authority document), establishment card, signed employment contract, and employee passport. TamkinExpress reviews all documents before any submission — eliminating MOHRE and ICA rejections from the first attempt.

2

Step 2: MOHRE Work Permit Application

TamkinExpress submits the MOHRE work permit for Fujairah — confirming the employer's legal right to hire under UAE labour law. Covers Fujairah DED mainland and FFZ / FOIZ (with correct specialist categories for FOIZ oil and energy roles). Typical timeline: 3–5 working days.

3

Step 3: ICA Employment Entry Permit

Employment entry permit issued by GDRFA/ICA — valid for 60 days from date of issue. Allows the employee to enter the UAE (outside country route) or change status (inside country route). TamkinExpress advises the optimal route for each Fujairah employee's situation. Typical: 2–5 working days.

4

Step 4: Medical Fitness Test & Emirates ID Biometrics

Mandatory medical fitness test at a government-approved UAE centre, followed by EIDA biometric registration. TamkinExpress coordinates both appointments for Fujairah employees. Typical: 3–10 working days.

5

Step 5: GDRFA Fujairah Residency Visa Stamping

A 2-year UAE residency visa stamped in the passport and digitally linked to the Emirates ID via GDRFA Fujairah. The employee is fully legal and authorised to work in Fujairah. Typical: 3–7 working days.

Processing Options

Inside Country vs Outside Country —
Fujairah Employment Visa

The most common question from Fujairah employers: does the employee need to leave the UAE to process their visa?

Inside country processing (change of status) converts the employee's existing visa to the new Fujairah employment visa without leaving the UAE — faster, no travel costs, no operational disruption. Not all visa types qualify. TamkinExpress checks eligibility before initiating.

Outside country processing requires the employee to exit the UAE and re-enter on the employment entry permit. Always available for all nationalities. Entry permit valid for 60 days from issuance. Standard for new hires joining from abroad.

TamkinExpress assesses each Fujairah employee's current visa status and advises the most cost-efficient and compliant route — no unnecessary travel, no wasted processing time.
