Overview

What is Business Registration in Umm Al Quwain?

Umm Al Quwain is consistently ranked among the most affordable UAE emirates for business formation — ideal for budget-conscious investors and startups.

Business registration in Umm Al Quwain (UAQ) means obtaining a trade license from UAQ DED for mainland businesses or from UAQ Free Trade Zone (UAQ FTZ) for freezone setups. UAQ FTZ is an increasingly popular choice offering 100% foreign ownership, multi-activity trade licenses, and flexible visa allocations at some of the UAE's lowest annual fees. TamkinExpress manages the complete registration process — from trade name reservation and initial approvals to MOA notarization and license delivery.
