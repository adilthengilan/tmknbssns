What is a CICPA Pass in Abu Dhabi?

CICPA stands for Critical Infrastructure and Coastal Protection Authority — the Abu Dhabi government body responsible for the security of the emirate's critical infrastructure zones, including oil and gas facilities, ports, coastal areas, and other protected sites.

A CICPA pass is a security clearance card that authorises individuals or vehicles to enter and operate within these restricted zones. Without a valid CICPA pass, access to these areas is strictly prohibited, and employees or vehicles found inside without one face serious legal consequences.

Any company sending workers or equipment to Abu Dhabi's protected zones — whether as a contractor, subcontractor, or service provider — is responsible for ensuring that all personnel and vehicles hold valid, up-to-date CICPA passes.

Important: CICPA passes are linked to the individual's residency visa and the sponsoring company's trade licence. If either expires, the pass becomes invalid. Timely renewal is critical to avoid access disruptions and compliance penalties.

Types of CICPA Passes in Abu Dhabi

There are four main categories of CICPA passes issued in Abu Dhabi, each designed for a specific type of access requirement:

Pass Type For Whom Validity Key Requirement
Employee Pass Workers employed at or regularly entering restricted zones 1 Year Valid UAE visa + Employer NOC
Vehicle Pass Cars, trucks, and heavy vehicles entering protected areas 1 Year Vehicle registration + Insurance
Contractor Pass Companies and project workers on temporary assignments Project duration Contract letter + Company documents
Visitor Pass Short-term or one-time access visitors Short-term Sponsoring company approval

Employee CICPA Pass

The most common type, issued to workers who regularly need access to critical infrastructure zones. This pass is tied to the employee's visa and must be renewed annually. The sponsoring company is responsible for applying and for cancelling the pass when the employee leaves.

Vehicle CICPA Pass

Required for any vehicle — car, SUV, truck, or heavy equipment — entering a CICPA-protected zone. The pass is linked to the vehicle's registration plate and must match the current registration and insurance status of the vehicle at all times.

Contractor CICPA Pass

Issued to companies and their workers who are engaged on specific contracts or projects within restricted areas. These passes are typically issued for the duration of the project and require a copy of the contract or work order as supporting documentation.

Who Needs a CICPA Pass in Abu Dhabi?

Any individual or vehicle requiring access to the following types of areas in Abu Dhabi will need a CICPA pass:

  • ADNOC oil and gas fields and refineries
  • Abu Dhabi ports and coastal protection zones
  • Military and defence installations
  • Power generation and utility infrastructure sites
  • Government-designated industrial and restricted zones
  • Musaffah industrial area (certain restricted sections)

Companies in construction, oil and gas services, logistics, maintenance, facility management, and security services most commonly need to manage CICPA passes for their workforce and vehicles.

Documents Required for a CICPA Pass

Having the correct documents ready is essential to avoid rejection and delays. Here is what is required for each pass type:

For Employee CICPA Pass:

  • Valid passport copy (minimum 6 months validity)
  • UAE Emirates ID copy
  • UAE residence visa copy (linked to sponsoring company)
  • Employer NOC letter on company letterhead
  • Sponsoring company trade licence copy
  • Passport-size photograph

For Vehicle CICPA Pass:

  • Vehicle registration card (Mulkiya) — valid and current
  • Valid UAE vehicle insurance certificate
  • Company NOC letter authorising the vehicle for zone entry
  • Driver's UAE driving licence copy (if applicable)

For Contractor CICPA Pass:

  • All employee pass documents (listed above)
  • Copy of the relevant contract or work order
  • Client company approval or site entry authorisation letter

CICPA Pass Renewal — What You Need to Know

CICPA passes are typically valid for one year. Renewal must be initiated before the pass expiry date — entering a restricted zone with an expired pass is a serious compliance violation and can result in access being permanently revoked for that individual or vehicle.

The renewal process requires updated copies of all supporting documents — particularly the UAE residence visa and vehicle registration, which must both be valid at the time of renewal. If the employee's visa has been renewed since the last CICPA application, the new visa copy must be submitted.
