Business Registration UAE 2025 — Complete Step by Step Guide

By Tamkin Express 📅 March 2026 ⏱ 8 min read

Registering a business in UAE has never been more accessible for foreign investors and entrepreneurs. With 100% foreign ownership now available in mainland UAE and dozens of free zones offering attractive packages, the UAE remains one of the world's top destinations for business setup.

This guide covers everything you need to know about business registration in UAE in 2025 — from choosing the right structure to understanding costs, documents, and timelines.

💡 Quick Summary: Business registration in UAE takes 3–14 days depending on structure. Costs start from AED 12,000 for free zone companies. Tamkin Express handles the complete process from consultation to licence delivery.

1. Types of Business Structures in UAE

Before registering a business in UAE, you need to choose the right legal structure. There are three main options:

A. Mainland Company Formation

A mainland company is registered with the Department of Economic Development (DED) in your emirate. Since the 2021 Commercial Companies Law amendment, 100% foreign ownership is now allowed for most business activities in mainland UAE.

  • Best for: Companies that want to trade anywhere in UAE including government contracts
  • Cost: AED 15,000 – AED 25,000+
  • Timeline: 7–14 working days

B. Free Zone Company Setup

Free zones are designated economic areas with special regulations. They offer 100% foreign ownership, zero corporate tax, and full profit repatriation.

  • Best for: International trade, import/export, tech, consulting
  • Cost: AED 12,000 – AED 20,000+
  • Timeline: 3–7 working days
  • Popular free zones: ADGM, JAFZA, DMCC, Meydan, RAKEZ

C. Offshore Company Registration

Offshore companies in UAE are used for international business, asset holding, and wealth management. They cannot conduct business within UAE.

  • Best for: Asset protection, international trading, holding companies
  • Cost: AED 8,000 – AED 15,000
  • Timeline: 3–5 working days

3. Documents Required for Business Registration in UAE

The exact documents depend on your business structure, but generally include:

  • Passport copies of all shareholders and managers
  • Emirates ID (for UAE residents)
  • Proposed trade name (3 options recommended)
  • Business activity description
  • Tenancy contract / Ejari (for mainland)
  • Memorandum of Association (MOA)
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) if currently employed in UAE

4. Step by Step Registration Process

  1. Choose business activity — Determine your trade licence category (commercial, professional, industrial)
  2. Select legal structure — Mainland, free zone, or offshore based on your needs
  3. Reserve trade name — Submit 3 name options to DED or free zone authority
  4. Prepare documents — MOA, shareholder documents, tenancy contract
  5. Submit application — To DED, free zone authority, or offshore registry
  6. Pay government fees — Trade licence fee + registration fee
  7. Receive trade licence — Company is registered and ready to operate
  8. Open corporate bank account — Required to operate business in UAE

5. Cost of Business Registration in UAE

Business registration costs in UAE vary significantly based on activity, location, and structure. Here is a general breakdown:

  • Trade licence fee: AED 3,000 – AED 10,000
  • Registration fee: AED 1,000 – AED 3,000
  • Office/flexi-desk fee: AED 3,000 – AED 15,000
  • Visa quota fees: AED 1,000 – AED 3,000 per visa
  • PRO/service fees: AED 2,000 – AED 5,000
