What is the difference between SEDD mainland and SAIF Zone PRO in Sharjah?

SEDD (Sharjah Economic Development Department) mainland companies are licensed by SEDD and can operate anywhere in Sharjah. PRO requirements include SEDD trade licence renewal, MOHRE work permits, and ICA residency visas. SAIF Zone, SHAMS, Hamriyah FZ, and Sharjah Publishing City companies are licensed by their respective free zone authority — the free zone handles the trade licence, while MOHRE and ICA processes remain federal for both.
