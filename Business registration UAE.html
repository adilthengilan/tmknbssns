Quick Answer

To register a business in the UAE, you choose an emirate and a license activity, reserve a trade name, obtain initial approval, register a tenancy contract for your premises, notarise a Memorandum of Association (for LLCs) and pay the government fees. Each emirate has its own licensing authority, such as ADDED in Abu Dhabi, Dubai Economy and Tourism in Dubai and SEDD in Sharjah. TamkinExpress manages the whole process for you from its Musaffah M43 office, and simple commercial licenses are often issued within 5 to 7 working days once documents are complete.

Mainland vs Free Zone

A mainland license lets you trade across the UAE market and work with government and corporate clients, and it requires registered premises in your emirate. A free zone license is issued by the free zone authority and usually limits direct trading in the local market. Many commercial and industrial activities now allow 100% foreign ownership on the mainland, while some regulated activities still carry ownership conditions. We confirm the rules for your activity before we file.

Process

How to Register a Business in the UAE: 5 Steps

We handle the complete registration process, from choosing your emirate and activity to receiving your license, in 5 steps.

Step 1: Choose Your Emirate and License Activity

We advise on the best emirate and license type for your business model, confirm the activity codes and check whether any activity needs external approval or has ownership conditions.

Step 2: Reserve the Trade Name

We check your preferred names against the local registry and reserve one with the licensing authority, making sure it follows UAE naming rules.

Step 3: Initial Approval and MOA

We obtain initial approval. For LLCs we prepare and notarise the Memorandum of Association, covering partner shares and capital.

Step 4: Premises, Approvals and Submission

We help you register the tenancy contract for your premises, collect any activity approvals, submit the full application through the government portal and pay the fees.

Step 5: License Issuance and Post-Setup

Your trade license is issued. We then assist with the establishment card, investor and employee visas, bank introduction letters and yearly renewals.
