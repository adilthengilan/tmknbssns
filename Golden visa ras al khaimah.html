What Is the UAE Golden Visa?

The UAE Golden Visa is a federal 10-year, self-sponsored residency introduced to attract investors, professionals, entrepreneurs, and students. Unlike an employer-sponsored visa, the Golden Visa requires no employer sponsorship — the holder can work for any employer, start a business, or remain unemployed without losing residency. No travel restriction and renewable indefinitely while the qualifying criterion is maintained.

Entrepreneurs with businesses in RAKEZ (Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone) may qualify for the UAE Golden Visa under the entrepreneur category. The primary eligibility criteria are: (1) the RAKEZ business meets the minimum annual revenue threshold required by ICA; (2) the business holds qualifying paid-up capital meeting ICA criteria; or (3) the business has received formal endorsement from an approved UAE incubator or accelerator program.
