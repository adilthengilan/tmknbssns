What is the difference between Ajman DED and AFZA PRO services?

Ajman DED (Department of Economic Development) mainland companies are licensed by Ajman DED and can operate anywhere in Ajman. PRO requirements include DED trade licence renewal, MOHRE work permits, and ICA residency visas. AFZA (Ajman Free Zone Authority) companies are licensed by AFZA — AFZA handles the trade licence, while MOHRE and ICA processes remain federal for both.
