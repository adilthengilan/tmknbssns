Employment Visa in Ajman UAE — Complete Guide 2025

Everything a business or employee in Ajman needs to know about the UAE employment visa process.

A UAE employment visa in Ajman allows a foreign national to legally live and work in the UAE under the sponsorship of an Ajman-registered employer. The employer is responsible for applying for, renewing, and cancelling the visa throughout the employee's tenure.

In Ajman, mainland companies registered with Ajman DED (Department of Economic Development) process work permits through MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation) and residence visas through GDRFA Ajman. Companies registered with the Ajman Free Zone Authority (AFZA) follow AFZA-specific work permit procedures before proceeding to the GDRFA Ajman residency visa stage.

How do I get an employment visa in Ajman in 2025?

To get an employment visa in Ajman in 2025: (1) The employer applies for a MOHRE work permit — for AFZA companies, work permits go through Ajman Free Zone Authority first. (2) An employment entry permit is issued via ICA. (3) The employee completes a medical fitness test at a government-approved centre. (4) Emirates ID biometrics are registered at EIDA. (5) The 2-year residence visa is stamped through GDRFA Ajman. The full process takes 2 to 4 weeks. TamkinExpress handles all five stages for Ajman employers.

What is the difference between Ajman mainland and AFZA employment visa?

For Ajman mainland companies (registered with Ajman DED), employment visa work permits are processed directly through MOHRE. For Ajman Free Zone (AFZA) companies, work permits are first processed through the Ajman Free Zone Authority before proceeding to the MOHRE and GDRFA Ajman stages. The residence visa stamping through GDRFA Ajman is the same for both routes. TamkinExpress handles both Ajman mainland and AFZA employment visa processing.
