Employment Visa in Ajman — What You Need to Know

Ajman is one of the most cost-effective emirates for business setup — and TamkinExpress makes visa processing just as simple.

A UAE employment visa allows a foreign national to legally live and work in the UAE under employer sponsorship. In Ajman, mainland companies process work permits through MOHRE and residence visas through GDRFA Ajman. Companies in the Ajman Free Zone (AFZA) follow AFZA procedures for work permits before GDRFA residency visa processing. TamkinExpress handles both routes — full compliance, end to end.

Ajman Specifics

Ajman Mainland vs Free Zone — Employment Visa Explained

The processing route differs depending on whether your company is registered on Ajman mainland or in the Ajman Free Zone.

🏢

Ajman Mainland Companies

MOHRE work permits and GDRFA Ajman residence visas for businesses registered with the Ajman Department of Economic Development. TamkinExpress handles all portal submissions.

🏙️

Ajman Free Zone (AFZA)

Employment visa processing for AFZA-registered businesses — full compliance with Ajman Free Zone Authority requirements before GDRFA residence visa stamping.

🔄

Inside vs Outside Country

Inside country processing changes visa status without leaving the UAE — faster and less disruptive. Outside country requires exit and re-entry. TamkinExpress advises the best route for your Ajman employee.

Cost-Effective Emirates
