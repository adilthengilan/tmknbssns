How do I get an employment visa in Ajman in 2025?

To get an employment visa in Ajman in 2025: (1) The employer applies for a MOHRE work permit — for AFZA companies, the work permit goes through Ajman Free Zone Authority first. (2) An employment entry permit is issued via ICA, valid for 60 days. (3) The employee completes a mandatory medical fitness test at a government-approved centre. (4) Emirates ID biometrics are registered at EIDA. (5) The 2-year residence visa is stamped through GDRFA Ajman. Total processing: 2 to 4 weeks. TamkinExpress manages all five stages for Ajman employers.

What is the difference between Ajman mainland and AFZA free zone employment visa?

For Ajman mainland companies (Ajman DED), work permits are processed directly through MOHRE. For Ajman Free Zone Authority (AFZA) companies, work permits are first processed through AFZA before proceeding to MOHRE and GDRFA Ajman stages. Residence visa stamping through GDRFA Ajman is the same for both routes. TamkinExpress handles both routes correctly.
