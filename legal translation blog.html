Legal translation in UAE is a mandatory requirement for many official documents submitted to government authorities, courts, and embassies. UAE law requires official documents to be in Arabic for submission to government bodies, making certified legal translation essential.

💡 Quick Summary: Legal translation in UAE requires certified translators approved by UAE Ministry of Justice. Documents for government use must be in Arabic. Tamkin Express provides certified legal translation services across all languages.

1. What is Legal Translation in UAE?

Legal translation is the certified translation of official documents — contracts, certificates, court documents, visa applications — by a UAE Ministry of Justice approved translator. Unlike general translation, legal translation carries official certification and is accepted by government authorities.

2. Documents That Require Legal Translation in UAE

  • Passports and Emirates ID — for government applications
  • Educational certificates — for employment, university admission
  • Marriage and birth certificates — for family visa, legal procedures
  • Business contracts — for court proceedings, government submission
  • Power of attorney — for notarization in UAE
  • Court documents — for legal proceedings
  • Medical reports — for insurance, legal claims
  • Company documents — MOA, trade licence for government use

3. Languages for Legal Translation in UAE

The most common language pairs for legal translation in UAE:

  • Arabic ↔ English — most common (government documents)
  • Arabic ↔ Hindi/Urdu — large expatriate community
  • Arabic ↔ French — North African nationals, European businesses
  • Arabic ↔ Chinese — growing Chinese investment community
  • English ↔ Other languages — for international use
