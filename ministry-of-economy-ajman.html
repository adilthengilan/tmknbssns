Ajman Business Landscape

Why Set Up a Business
in Ajman, UAE?

Ajman is the UAE's smallest emirate by area, yet it punches well above its weight as a business destination. Known across the UAE business community for offering the lowest trade license fees and commercial rents of any mainland UAE emirate, Ajman has become the go-to choice for cost-conscious entrepreneurs, startups, SMEs, and trading businesses looking for a fully legitimate UAE mainland presence at a fraction of the cost of Abu Dhabi or Dubai.

The Ajman Department of Economic Development (Ajman DED) serves as the emirate-level licensing authority, working alongside the federal Ministry of Economy UAE which governs commercial registration, trademark protection, and national-level business approvals. Businesses operating on the Ajman mainland require compliance with both authorities, and Tamkin Express coordinates the two registration tracks simultaneously to eliminate delays and reduce your time to license.

Ajman's geographical position — sharing borders with Sharjah and just 25 kilometres from central Dubai — gives businesses registered in Ajman seamless access to the broader Northern Emirates and Greater Dubai market. Many entrepreneurs deliberately choose Ajman as their licensing emirate while conducting business actively across Dubai and Sharjah, and Tamkin Express regularly advises on the optimal multi-emirate business structure for such scenarios.

Ajman's economy is anchored by manufacturing, particularly in the construction materials, furniture, fishing, and light industrial sectors. The Ajman Free Zone (AFZ) is one of the UAE's oldest and most cost-effective free zones, while Ajman's mainland offers businesses direct access to the UAE domestic market without free zone operational restrictions. Tamkin Express advises on the advantages and limitations of each registration option for businesses considering Ajman.

The UAE's 2021 foreign ownership reforms have opened Ajman's mainland to 100% foreign ownership across many business activity categories, removing the historic requirement for a UAE national partner in most commercial, professional, and industrial activities. This change has driven a significant increase in foreign entrepreneur registrations in Ajman, and Tamkin Express has guided many international clients through the updated ownership framework.

Despite its compact size, Ajman is home to a thriving port — the Port of Ajman — which supports trading, fishing, and light industry businesses. Whether you are a first-time business owner seeking the most affordable UAE trade license, or an established company looking to add an Ajman entity to your UAE corporate structure, Tamkin Express provides expert end-to-end support. Document Checklist — Ajman

Documents Required for
Ajman Business Registration

Correct documentation is the single most important factor in achieving first-time Ajman DED and Ministry of Economy approval. Requirements vary by license type and business structure.

✓
Passport CopiesValid passport copies for all shareholders and managers. UAE-resident shareholders also need a copy of their current valid residence visa page included in the submission.
✓
Emirates IDCurrent Emirates ID for all UAE-resident shareholders and directors. Non-resident shareholders may require a notarised passport copy with UAE entry stamp for Ajman DED submission.
✓
Trade Name Approval — Ajman DEDAn approved trade name reservation from the Ajman Department of Economic Development. Names must be unique, comply with UAE naming conventions, and must not reference government bodies or religions without special approval.
✓
Memorandum of AssociationA legally drafted and notarised MOA specifying shareholders, ownership percentages, authorised capital, and the permitted scope of business activities — attested by a UAE Notary Public before submission.
✓
Ajman Tenancy ContractA valid tenancy contract for an Ajman business address appropriate for your licensed activity type. Premises must match the commercial, professional, or industrial nature of the license being applied for.
✓
No Objection Certificate (NOC)Required if any shareholder or manager is sponsored by an existing UAE employer — ensuring compliance with UAE labour and residency regulations prior to Ajman DED and Ministry of Economy submission.
✓
Initial Approval — Ajman DED & MinistryInitial approvals from the Ajman DED and, where applicable, the Ministry of Economy must be obtained prior to final license issuance. Tamkin Express manages this step on your behalf to avoid common delays.
✓
Sector-Specific Ajman ApprovalsFood businesses require Ajman Municipality health clearance; construction requires municipality approval; healthcare requires health authority clearance. Tamkin Express identifies every required sector approval before submission.
