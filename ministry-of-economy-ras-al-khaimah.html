Business in RAK

Why Set Up a Business in Ras Al Khaimah

Ras Al Khaimah has emerged as one of the UAE's most attractive destinations for business formation. With significantly lower setup and operational costs compared to Dubai and Abu Dhabi, RAK offers entrepreneurs and investors a compelling combination of strategic location, business-friendly regulations, and direct access to both regional and global markets via RAK's deep-water port and international airport.

The RAK Department of Economic Development (RAK DED) handles emirate-level trade licensing for mainland businesses in Ras Al Khaimah. For most businesses, obtaining a RAK mainland trade license also requires coordination with the federal Ministry of Economy for commercial registration, trademark protection, and sector-specific compliance approvals. Both layers of registration must be correctly completed for your business to operate legally in RAK.

Ras Al Khaimah is also home to the RAK Economic Zone (RAKEZ), one of the UAE's leading free zones catering to industrial, commercial, and professional businesses. While free zone companies operate under RAKEZ regulations, any business that wants to conduct trade directly with the RAK mainland market will require a Ministry of Economy registered mainland license. Tamkin Express advises on the optimal structure for your specific business goals.

Key sectors driving RAK's economy include ceramics and manufacturing, tourism and hospitality, real estate and construction, logistics and trade, and a fast-growing SME business community. Each of these sectors has distinct Ministry of Economy licensing requirements, and Tamkin Express has experience across all of them.

Since the UAE's 2021 foreign ownership reforms, many business activities in Ras Al Khaimah's mainland now permit 100% foreign ownership without requiring a local UAE sponsor. This has made RAK an increasingly popular choice for international entrepreneurs looking to establish a UAE presence with full ownership and lower overhead than the larger emirates.

Whether you are a first-time entrepreneur launching in RAK, an existing business looking to expand from another emirate, or an international company establishing a UAE mainland presence, Tamkin Express provides end-to-end Ministry of Economy services tailored to the Ras Al Khaimah business environment. Contact our team today for a free consultation specific to your RAK business requirements.

Document Checklist — RAK

Documents Required for RAK Trade License Registration

Accurate documentation is essential for a fast, first-time approval from RAK DED and the Ministry of Economy. Requirements below apply to most RAK mainland business formations.

✓
Passport CopiesValid passport copies for all shareholders and managers. UAE resident shareholders must also provide the residence visa page.
✓
Emirates IDCurrent Emirates ID for all UAE-resident shareholders and directors. Non-residents may need a notarised passport copy with entry stamp.
✓
Trade Name Approval — RAK DEDApproved trade name reservation from RAK DED. Names must comply with UAE naming conventions and be unique within the RAK registry.
✓
Memorandum of AssociationDrafted, attested, and notarised MOA outlining shareholder details, ownership percentages, and business activities as registered with RAK DED.
✓
Tenancy Contract — RAK PremisesValid tenancy contract for a RAK business address appropriate for the licensed activity. Tamkin Express can advise on RAK office solutions if needed.
✓
No Objection Certificate (NOC)Required if any shareholder is currently sponsored by a UAE employer. Ensures compliance with UAE labour and residency regulations for RAK-based business formation.
✓
Initial Approval — RAK DEDInitial approval certificate from RAK DED must be obtained before Ministry of Economy federal submission. Tamkin Express manages this step simultaneously to save time.
✓
RAK Sector-Specific ApprovalsIndustrial businesses need RAK Municipality approval. Food businesses require RAK Health Authority clearance. Tourism operators need RAK Tourism approvals. We coordinate all required clearances.

RAK-Specific Note from Tamkin Express: RAK DED document requirements and processing timelines can differ from Abu Dhabi and Dubai procedures. Our team is fully familiar with RAK-specific processes and maintains up-to-date knowledge of current RAK DED requirements to ensure your application moves through the system without unnecessary delays.
