Overview

What Are Notary & Court Services in Dubai?

In Dubai and across the UAE, notarized documents are a legal requirement for hundreds of personal, business, and property transactions — without notarization, documents have no legal standing.

Notary and court services in Dubai refer to the official authentication, certification, and legal attestation of documents through the UAE Ministry of Justice, Dubai Court Notary Public offices, or federal notary authorities. These services are legally required before a document is recognized by UAE government bodies, banks, courts, real estate registries (Dubai Land Department), or international embassies.

Documents that require notarization in Dubai include Power of Attorney (POA), Memorandum of Association (MOA), tenancy contracts, sale and purchase agreements, affidavits, statutory declarations, personal status documents (marriage, divorce, birth certificates), and educational certificates. Without proper notarization through a UAE Notary Public, these documents carry no legal standing in the UAE.

For documents used outside the UAE, attestation services are additionally required — a three-stage chain: UAE Ministry of Justice → Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) → relevant embassy or consulate. TamkinExpress manages this complete chain in Dubai on behalf of clients.

TamkinExpress provides end-to-end notary and court services in Dubai — handling document preparation, Arabic translation where required, official submission, active follow-up, and certified delivery through official channels — so clients never need to visit Dubai Court or queue at government offices.

Notarization is performed by a UAE Notary Public (typically through Dubai Court) and serves domestic UAE purposes. It verifies the authenticity of a document's content, confirms the identity of the signing parties, and officially records the document with the UAE Ministry of Justice. Notarization is required for Power of Attorney, property transactions, business agreements, and court proceedings within the UAE.

Attestation is an additional endorsement process performed by government authorities — UAE Ministry of Justice, MOFA, or a foreign embassy — that enables a notarized document to be legally recognized in another country. Attestation is required any time a UAE-notarized document needs to be used internationally.

In practice: notarization covers UAE domestic legal use; attestation covers international recognition. Many documents require both processes. TamkinExpress handles both notarization and attestation in Dubai and advises which process your specific document requires.
