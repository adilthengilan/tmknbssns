Fujairah Business Landscape

Why Set Up a Business
in Fujairah, UAE?

Fujairah is the UAE's only emirate situated entirely on the Arabian Sea coast, facing the Gulf of Oman. This geographic advantage has made it one of the Middle East's fastest-growing commercial hubs for port trade, oil and gas storage, maritime services, construction, and tourism. For entrepreneurs and investors seeking a mainland UAE base with direct east coast access and lower operating costs than Abu Dhabi or Dubai, Fujairah presents compelling advantages.

The Fujairah Economic Development Department (FEDD) serves as the emirate-level licensing authority, working alongside the federal Ministry of Economy UAE to regulate commercial activity. Every business seeking to operate on the Fujairah mainland must satisfy both layers — the FEDD trade license and, where required, federal Ministry of Economy approvals. Navigating these parallel processes efficiently is exactly where Tamkin Express delivers measurable value.

Fujairah's Port is one of the world's largest bunkering hubs, handling millions of tonnes of cargo annually. This makes the emirate an ideal location for trading companies, freight forwarders, ship suppliers, and logistics operators who need seamless east coast access to global shipping lanes without routing through the Strait of Hormuz — a key strategic differentiator for internationally active businesses.

The UAE's 2021 foreign ownership reforms have significantly benefited Fujairah-based businesses. Many business activity categories now allow 100% foreign ownership on the Fujairah mainland, removing the historic requirement to partner with a UAE national for most commercial and professional activity types. Tamkin Express has supported many international businesses in structuring their Fujairah entities correctly under this updated framework.

Fujairah's growing sectors — oil storage and bunkering, quarrying and stone processing, hospitality and eco-tourism, fishing and aquaculture, and professional services — each carry sector-specific licensing requirements that go beyond a standard trade license. Certain activities require approvals from the Port Authority, Fujairah Municipality, tourism authorities, or the Ministry of Energy. Tamkin Express identifies and coordinates every required approval before your application is submitted to ensure first-time success.

Whether you are launching a new venture, expanding an existing UAE business into Fujairah, or a foreign investor entering the UAE market through the east coast, Tamkin Express provides a single point of contact for all Ministry of Economy and FEDD requirements — from initial consultation to license delivery and ongoing renewal. Document Checklist — Fujairah

Documents Required for
Fujairah Business Registration

Accurate documentation is the single biggest factor in achieving first-time Ministry of Economy and FEDD approval. Exact requirements vary by license type and business structure.

✓
Passport CopiesValid passport copies for all shareholders and managers. UAE-resident shareholders also need a copy of their residence visa page.
✓
Emirates IDCurrent Emirates ID for all UAE-resident shareholders and directors. Non-resident shareholders may require a notarised passport copy with entry stamp.
✓
Trade Name Approval — FEDDApproved trade name reservation from the Fujairah Economic Development Department. Names must be unique and comply with UAE naming conventions and FEDD guidelines.
✓
Memorandum of AssociationA legally drafted and notarised MOA outlining shareholders, ownership percentages, capital structure, and permitted business activities — attested by a UAE Notary Public.
✓
Fujairah Tenancy ContractA valid tenancy contract for a Fujairah business address appropriate for your licensed activity. Residential addresses are not accepted for commercial or industrial license types.
✓
No Objection Certificate (NOC)Required if any shareholder or manager is currently sponsored by a UAE employer. Ensures compliance with UAE labour and residency regulations before FEDD submission.
✓
Initial Approvals — FEDD & MinistryInitial approvals from FEDD and, where applicable, the Ministry of Economy must be obtained before final license issuance. Tamkin Express manages this step on your behalf.
✓
Sector-Specific Fujairah ApprovalsDepending on your activity, additional approvals may be needed from the Fujairah Port Authority, Municipality, Energy Authority, or Tourism and Antiquities Authority. We coordinate all required clearances.
