What is an Employment Visa in UAE?

An employment visa in UAE — also known as a work residence visa — is the official authorisation allowing a foreign national to legally live and work in the UAE. It is tied to a specific employer and job role, and consists of two mandatory components:

  • Work Permit (MOHRE) — Issued by the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation. Authorises the employee to legally work for a specific employer in UAE mainland.
  • Residence Visa (ICA) — Issued by the Federal Authority for Identity and Citizenship. Authorises the employee to legally reside in UAE for the duration of employment.

Both approvals are mandatory for every employee working in UAE mainland. Free zone employees follow a slightly different process through their respective free zone authority.

Who Needs an Employment Visa in UAE?

Every foreign national seeking employment in UAE mainland must have a valid employment visa before legally beginning work. This includes:

  • New employees being hired from outside UAE
  • Employees already in UAE on a tourist or visit visa being hired by a UAE company
  • Employees transferring from one UAE company to another
  • Employees renewing their existing employment visa upon expiry

Note: UAE nationals are exempt from the employment visa requirement. GCC nationals have a simplified process. All other nationalities must follow the standard UAE employment visa process through MOHRE and ICA.

Employment Visa UAE — Step by Step Process

The UAE employment visa process consists of six key steps. Understanding each step helps you prepare the right documents and avoid delays.

01
Work Permit Application — MOHRE

The employer submits a work permit application through the MOHRE portal. This requires the employee's passport, attested educational certificates, and a signed labour contract. Approval typically takes 24–48 hours.

02
Entry Permit Issuance

Once the work permit is approved, an entry permit is issued by ICA — allowing the employee to enter UAE for employment purposes if they are outside the country. Entry permits are valid for 60 days.

03
Status Change (if already in UAE)

If the employee is already in UAE on a tourist or visit visa, a status change application converts their status to a residence visa — avoiding the need to exit and re-enter the country.

04
Medical Fitness Test

All employees must undergo a medical fitness test at a UAE government-approved health centre screening for communicable diseases. Results are typically available within 24–48 hours.

05
Emirates ID Registration

Biometric registration and Emirates ID application is submitted through the Federal Authority for Identity and Citizenship — the official UAE identification document for the employee.

06
Residence Visa Stamping

The final residence visa is stamped in the employee's passport — completing the employment visa process. The visa is valid for 2 years and must be renewed before expiry.

Documents Required for Employment Visa in UAE

Employee Documents

  • Original passport — valid for minimum 6 months
  • Passport-size photograph — white background, recent
  • Educational certificates — attested by UAE MOFA or Ministry of Education
  • Previous UAE visa copy — if the employee has been in UAE before
  • Medical fitness test results from a UAE-approved health centre

Employer Documents

  • Valid UAE trade licence — copy of current licence
  • Signed labour contract — MOHRE-approved employment contract
  • MOHRE establishment card of the company
  • Authorised signatory's Emirates ID copy

Employment Visa Renewal in UAE

UAE employment visas are valid for 2 years and must be renewed before expiry to avoid overstay fines and legal complications.

  • Renewal application should be submitted at least 30 days before expiry
  • Overstay fines begin at AED 200 per day after visa expiry date
  • Employees must not leave UAE on an expired visa
  • Renewal requires a new medical fitness test and updated documents

Employment Visa Cancellation in UAE

When employment ends, the visa must be officially cancelled through MOHRE and ICA. The process includes:

  • Cancellation of the work permit through MOHRE
  • Cancellation of the residence visa through ICA
  • Settlement of outstanding dues as per the labour contract
  • Employee must leave UAE or obtain a new visa within the 30-day grace period

Family Visa Sponsorship

Employees holding a UAE employment visa can sponsor family members for UAE residence, subject to minimum salary requirements:

  • Spouse sponsorship: Minimum salary of AED 4,000/month or AED 3,000 + accommodation
  • Children sponsorship: No minimum salary — boys up to age 18, daughters until marriage
  • Parents sponsorship: Minimum salary of AED 20,000/month

Common Rejection Reasons & How to Avoid Them

The most frequent causes of UAE employment visa rejection include:

  • Unattested educational certificates — the #1 rejection reason
  • Passport with less than 6 months validity
  • Incorrect or unsigned labour contract
  • Medical fitness test failure
  • Employer visa quota exceeded
  • Expired MOHRE establishment card
