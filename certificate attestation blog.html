Certificate Attestation UAE 2025 — Complete Step by Step Guide | Tamkin Express

By Tamkin Express 📅 March 2026 ⏱ 7 min read

Certificate attestation in UAE is the process of officially verifying documents so they are legally recognized by UAE government authorities, employers, and educational institutions. Whether you are applying for an employment visa, enrolling in a UAE university, or starting a business, document attestation is a mandatory requirement.

💡 Quick Summary: Certificate attestation in UAE involves notarization in home country, foreign affairs ministry attestation, UAE embassy attestation, then MOFA UAE attestation. Tamkin Express handles the complete attestation process.

1. What is Certificate Attestation in UAE?

Certificate attestation is the multi-step process of verifying the authenticity of documents issued outside UAE so they are accepted by UAE government authorities. It involves authentication by multiple bodies in both the home country and UAE.

2. Documents That Require Attestation in UAE

Educational Certificates

  • Degree certificates and transcripts
  • Diplomas and professional qualifications
  • School leaving certificates
  • Mark sheets and grade cards

Personal Documents

  • Birth certificates
  • Marriage certificates
  • Death certificates
  • Police clearance certificates
  • Medical certificates

Commercial Documents

  • Company incorporation certificates
  • Board resolutions
  • Power of attorney
  • Commercial invoices and agreements

3. Certificate Attestation Process — Step by Step

  1. Notarization — Document notarized by a registered notary in home country
  2. Home country foreign affairs attestation — Ministry of Foreign Affairs in home country authenticates
  3. UAE Embassy attestation — UAE Embassy in home country certifies the document
  4. MOFA UAE attestation — UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) provides final attestation
  5. Optional: Ministry attestation — Additional attestation by relevant UAE ministry if required

5. Apostille vs Attestation

Some countries are members of the Hague Apostille Convention — for documents from these countries, an apostille stamp replaces the multi-step attestation process, making it faster and cheaper. UAE is not a member, so full attestation is required for UAE use. However, documents from UAE going to apostille member countries can use the apostille process.

6. Tips to Avoid Attestation Delays

  • Ensure documents are original or properly certified copies
  • Check that names match exactly across all documents
  • Translate documents to Arabic before UAE MOFA attestation if required
  • Start the process early — attestation can take 4–8 weeks for some countries
  • Use a professional service like Tamkin Express to avoid errors and rejections
