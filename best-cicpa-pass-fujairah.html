Overview

What is a CICPA Pass?

If your workers need to enter Fujairah's industrial and coastal protection zones, a CICPA pass is not optional — it's mandatory.

A CICPA pass (Critical Infrastructure and Coastal Protection Authority pass) is a government-issued security clearance required for workers and visitors entering designated industrial and critical infrastructure zones in the UAE — including Fujairah's industrial and coastal protection zones. Without a valid CICPA pass, workers cannot legally enter or operate within these zones.
