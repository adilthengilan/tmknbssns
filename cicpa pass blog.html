What is a CICPA Pass in Abu Dhabi?

CICPA stands for Critical Infrastructure and Coastal Protection Authority — the Abu Dhabi authority historically responsible for the security of the emirate's critical infrastructure, including oil and gas facilities, ports, coastal zones, and other protected sites.

A CICPA pass is the security clearance card that authorises an individual, vehicle, or vessel to enter and operate within these restricted zones. Without a valid pass, access is strictly prohibited, and anyone found inside without one can face serious compliance consequences for both the individual and the sponsoring company.

Name change to know about: CICPA's responsibilities have been restructured under the UAE National Guard Command. In practice, government documents, PRO offices, and most companies still refer to these permits as "CICPA passes" (sometimes "CNIA passes," from CICPA's earlier name, Critical National Infrastructure Authority), so both terms remain in everyday use. We use "CICPA pass" throughout this guide for that reason.

Any company sending workers, vehicles, or equipment into Abu Dhabi's protected zones — whether as a direct contractor, subcontractor, or service provider — is responsible for ensuring that everyone and everything entering the zone holds a valid pass.

Types of CICPA Passes in Abu Dhabi

CICPA passes are issued at the individual, vehicle, or vessel level, and every pass is applied for separately. In day-to-day use, companies generally encounter five categories:

Pass Type For Whom Typical Use
Permanent Land Pass Employees who regularly enter a restricted land site Long-term site staff, technicians, engineers
Vehicle Pass Cars, trucks, and heavy vehicles Tied to a specific plate number and the company's LOA
Vessel / Marine Pass Boats and vessels entering coastal or marine zones Offshore and marine operations
Temporary / Non-Permanent Pass Short-term contractors and project workers Issued for the duration of a specific contract or task
Photography Permit Anyone filming or photographing inside a restricted zone Requires a separate, specific approval

Permanent Land Pass (Employee Pass)

The most common type, issued to staff who regularly need access to a critical-infrastructure site. It is tied to the employee's UAE residence visa, which must be sponsored by the company holding the LOA for that site — the sponsoring company is also responsible for cancelling the pass when the employee leaves.

Vehicle Pass

Required for any vehicle — car, SUV, truck, or heavy equipment — entering a CICPA-protected zone. The pass is linked to the vehicle's registration plate and must match the vehicle's current Mulkiya (registration) and insurance status, and the number/category of vehicles must be covered under the company's LOA.

Temporary / Non-Permanent & Contractor Passes

Issued to companies and workers engaged on a specific contract or project within a restricted area. These are usually issued for the duration of that contract and require a copy of the relevant contract or client authorisation as supporting documentation.

Who Needs a CICPA Pass in Abu Dhabi?

Anyone — and any vehicle or vessel — requiring access to Abu Dhabi's critical-infrastructure zones needs a CICPA pass. This commonly includes:

  • ADNOC oil and gas fields, refineries, and offshore rigs
  • Abu Dhabi ports and coastal protection zones
  • Power generation and utility infrastructure sites
  • Aviation and select transport (air, land, maritime, and rail) facilities
  • Water, health, finance, and communications sites classified as critical infrastructure
  • Suppliers in the food-supply chain that feed into these sectors

Companies in construction, oil and gas services, logistics, maintenance, facility management, manpower supply, and security services most commonly need to manage CICPA passes for their workforce, vehicles, and vessels.

Who is NOT Eligible for a CICPA Pass?

Eligibility is one of the most overlooked parts of the CICPA process — and getting it wrong is a common reason applications are rejected outright. The following are generally not eligible:

  • Free-zone visa holders (with limited exceptions for select zones such as Masdar City and twofour54, subject to additional approval)
  • Visit or tourist visa holders
  • Residence visas issued from an emirate other than Abu Dhabi
  • Temporary MOHRE work permit holders (3-month, 6-month, or 1-year permits)

In practice, this means the employee's UAE residence visa must be sponsored by an Abu Dhabi mainland company that holds — or is covered under — a valid LOA for the relevant site. Checking this upfront avoids wasted submissions and delays.

Documents Required for a CICPA Pass

CICPA applications involve both company-level approvals and individual-level documents. Missing either causes the most common rejections.

Company-Level Requirements (Before Applying)

  • Valid Abu Dhabi mainland trade/economic licence
  • Registration with the Abu Dhabi Chamber of Commerce and Industry (ADCCI)
  • Supreme Petroleum Council (SPC) approval — required for oil and gas activity
  • Letter of Assistance (LOA) from ADNOC or the relevant concession-holding company
  • A registered, company-sponsored PRO (Public Relations Officer) to submit applications

For Individual / Employee Passes:

  • Valid passport copy
  • UAE Emirates ID copy
  • UAE residence visa copy (sponsored by the LOA-holding company)
  • Passport-style photograph (4cm x 6cm, white background)
  • PRO's Emirates ID, passport, visa, and labour card copies

For Vehicle Passes:

  • Vehicle registration card (Mulkiya) — valid and current
  • Valid UAE vehicle insurance certificate
  • No-objection letter from the traffic and licensing department, with vehicle plate numbers and categories
  • Driver's UAE driving licence copy (if applicable)

For Vessel / Marine Passes:

  • Vessel registration and ownership documents
  • Crew details and relevant visa documentation
  • LOA coverage confirming the vessel's permitted area of operation

How to Apply for a CICPA Pass in Abu Dhabi — Step by Step

The CICPA pass process is a structured, multi-stage approval flow rather than a single form. Working with a professional PRO service like TamkinExpress ensures each stage is completed correctly and in the right order.

02
Register the Company (One-Time Step)

Register the company using the mainland trade licence and ADCCI registration to receive an Establishment Security Code. For most companies, this registration only needs to be done once.

Individuals cannot apply directly — a company-sponsored PRO (commonly an Arab national) must be registered to handle submissions. TamkinExpress can provide PRO support if your company doesn't have one in place.

05

Applications are submitted through the official Permit Program in Arabic only, with full supporting documents for every individual, vehicle, or vessel covered under the LOA. TamkinExpress prepares and submits this on your behalf.

06
Security Clearance & Pass Issuance

Once security clearance is complete, the pass is issued. TamkinExpress tracks the application, handles any follow-up queries, and collects and delivers the approved pass to you.

CICPA Pass Renewal & Validity — What You Need to Know

This is the part most guides get wrong: a CICPA pass does not simply renew every 12 months like a standard ID card.

Pro Tip: Company registration with the authority is usually a one-time process. What actually expires is the Letter of Assistance (LOA) and the PRO card linked to it. The moment the LOA expires, every CICPA pass issued under it expires too — regardless of how recently that specific pass was issued. "Renewal" really means: re-securing the LOA, reissuing the PRO card, and then reapplying for the individual passes under the new LOA.

Practically, this means renewal timelines are driven by the LOA's expiry date and the concession holder's (e.g. ADNOC's) own approval cycle — not by a fixed calendar date you set yourself. TamkinExpress tracks LOA and PRO card expiry for clients we manage, and initiates renewal proactively so passes don't lapse mid-project.

Processing Time & Costs

There is no single official turnaround time published for CICPA passes — the real driver is how quickly the LOA and (where relevant) SPC approval move, since the pass application itself can't proceed without them.

  • Biggest variable: LOA approval time from ADNOC or the concession holder — this is usually the longest step in the process
  • Secondary variable: SPC approval, for oil and gas-related activity
  • Avoidable delay: incomplete or mismatched documents at the individual-pass stage, which trigger resubmission cycles
  • Government fees: vary by pass type, category, and duration — contact TamkinExpress for the current fee schedule

Pro Tip: The single biggest cause of CICPA pass delays isn't the government step — it's document errors on the company's side: expired visa copies, mismatched names, an LOA that doesn't cover the right vehicles or headcount, or missing PRO authorisation.
