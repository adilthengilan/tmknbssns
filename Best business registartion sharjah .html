Business Registration in Sharjah — What It Means

Sharjah is a key commercial and industrial hub in the UAE. Every business operating here requires a valid SEDD trade license — and TamkinExpress navigates that process for you.

What Is a SEDD Trade License?

The Sharjah Economic Development Department (SEDD) is the licensing authority for all commercial, industrial, professional, and workshop activities on the Sharjah mainland. A valid SEDD trade license is legally required for every business operating in Sharjah — from a small workshop to a large multi-partner LLC. Sharjah also hosts several major free zones — SHAMS, Hamriyah, and SAIF Zone — offering 100% foreign ownership across diverse activities.

SHAMS — Sharjah Media City, also known as Sharjah Publishing City — is the Arab world's largest publishing and media free zone. TamkinExpress provides full SHAMS setup services.
SAIF Zone (Sharjah Airport International Free Zone) is located adjacent to Sharjah International Airport and offers state-of-the-art warehousing, industrial units, and office spaces. Hamriyah Free Zone Authority (HFZA) is located adjacent to Hamriyah Port — Sharjah's major industrial seaport. It offers industrial land plots, factory units, warehouses, and office facilities with direct port access. It is particularly popular with manufacturing, construction materials, chemicals, and oil & gas support businesses.
