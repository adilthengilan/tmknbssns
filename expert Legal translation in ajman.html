Legal Translation in Ajman — What It Means

Every document submitted to Ajman government authorities, courts, embassies, or employers must be translated by a certified legal translator. TamkinExpress provides that service with fast delivery across Ajman.

What Is Certified Legal Translation?

Legal translation is the certified conversion of official documents from one language to another by a licensed translator. In Ajman, certified translation is required for submitting documents to Ajman courts, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA), embassies, Ajman DED, and government departments. A translated document is only legally valid when it carries the translator's official certification stamp and signature — a plain translation is not accepted.
