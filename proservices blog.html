What is a PRO in UAE — and What Do They Do?

PRO stands for Public Relations Officer. In the UAE context, a PRO is a licensed professional who acts as the official liaison between a company and the various government authorities, ministries, and regulatory bodies that every UAE business must interact with.

A PRO handles all government-related paperwork, submissions, and follow-ups — from employment visa applications filed with MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation) to trade licence renewals processed through the Department of Economic Development, and from document attestation to notary services.

Larger companies often employ an in-house PRO officer. Smaller and medium-sized businesses typically outsource PRO functions to a professional PRO services company in UAE like TamkinExpress — which is more cost-efficient than a full-time hire while delivering expert, dedicated service.

Key point: In the UAE, government authority processes — visa submissions, MOHRE portals, trade licence portals, immigration systems — each have their own procedures, formats, and requirements. A professional PRO service has established expertise and relationships across all these systems, preventing the errors and delays that in-house staff without this specialised knowledge frequently cause.

Visa Processing — The Core PRO Service

The most frequently used PRO service for UAE businesses is employment visa processing. Every new employee hired from outside the UAE requires an employment visa — a multi-stage process involving MOHRE, GDRFA (General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs), and EIDA (Emirates Identity Authority).

A professional PRO service handles the complete employment visa process — from the initial work permit application through MOHRE, to the entry permit, medical fitness test coordination, Emirates ID biometrics, and final residency visa stamping. TamkinExpress manages every stage, tracking applications and handling any government queries on your behalf.

MOHRE & Labour Documentation Services

MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation) is the central authority governing the UAE labour market. Every mainland employer must register with MOHRE and all employment-related transactions — work permits, employment contracts, labour cards, and WPS compliance — are managed through MOHRE systems.

Navigating the MOHRE portal correctly requires detailed knowledge of the submission formats, required fields, and document specifications for each transaction type. Errors in MOHRE submissions are a frequent cause of visa delays and labour violations for businesses without professional PRO support.

MOHRE Services Handled:

  • Work permit applications — new and renewal
  • Employment contract registration and amendments
  • Labour card processing and renewals
  • MOHRE establishment card maintenance
  • WPS (Wage Protection System) registration and compliance
  • Labour inspection preparation and compliance documentation
  • Quota management — employee visa quota applications and increases

Important: Non-compliance with MOHRE regulations — including WPS salary payment violations, expired work permits, or unregistered employment contracts — can result in significant fines and restrictions on issuing new visas. TamkinExpress ensures all MOHRE obligations are met on time, every time.

Trade Licence Renewal & Business Compliance

A UAE trade licence must be renewed annually. Allowing a trade licence to lapse — even briefly — has serious consequences: the business cannot legally operate, bank accounts may be frozen, and new visa applications are blocked until the licence is reinstated.

Trade licence renewal involves submitting updated documents to the relevant DED (Department of Economic Development) or free zone authority, paying government fees, and in some cases renewing the office tenancy contract (Ejari) simultaneously. TamkinExpress tracks all client renewal dates and proactively initiates the renewal process well before the expiry date.

Business Compliance Services Handled:

  • Trade licence renewal — mainland (ADDED, DED Dubai) and all free zones
  • Chamber of Commerce membership renewal
  • Ejari and tenancy contract renewal coordination
  • Company activity amendments and additions
  • Company name change applications
  • Shareholder amendments and MOA modifications
  • Establishment card renewal
