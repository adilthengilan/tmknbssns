Business Registration in Dubai — What It Means

Dubai is a key commercial and industrial hub in the UAE. Every business operating here requires a valid Dubai DED trade license — and TamkinExpress navigates that process for you.

What Is a Dubai DED Trade License?

The Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism (DET) — commonly called Dubai DED — issues all mainland commercial, professional, industrial, and tourism licenses in Dubai. A DED license is mandatory for any legal mainland business operation: from a single-owner consultancy to a multi-partner LLC. It unlocks bank accounts, employee visas, and the ability to trade directly with UAE government entities.
