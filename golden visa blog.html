What is the UAE Golden Visa?

The UAE Golden Visa is a long-term residence visa introduced by the UAE government to attract and retain high-value talent and investors. Unlike a standard UAE residence visa — which requires a UAE employer as sponsor and is valid for 2 to 3 years — the Golden Visa is valid for 10 years, is self-sponsored, and is renewable.

Golden Visa holders can live, work, start businesses, and study in the UAE without needing an employer sponsor. They can also sponsor their immediate family members — spouse, children, and parents — under the same long-term residency status.

Since its expansion in 2022, the UAE Golden Visa programme has broadened its eligibility criteria significantly, making it accessible to a much wider range of professionals, investors, and skilled workers than when it was first introduced in 2019.

Key benefit: A UAE Golden Visa does not expire if the holder leaves the country for an extended period — unlike a standard UAE residence visa, which becomes invalid after 6 months outside the UAE. Golden Visa holders can travel freely and return to the UAE at any time within the 10-year validity period.

Investors

Investors can qualify for the UAE Golden Visa by making a qualifying investment of AED 2 million or more in UAE real estate — purchased outright or through a mortgage with AED 2 million already paid — or through approved business investments in UAE-registered companies. Investment funds and approved financial products may also qualify. TamkinExpress verifies investment eligibility before application to ensure a successful outcome.

Entrepreneurs

UAE-based entrepreneurs whose companies are registered with a UAE trade licence and generating qualifying annual revenue, or whose businesses have been endorsed by a UAE accredited business incubator, can apply for the Golden Visa entrepreneur category. Founders of startups recognised by UAE government-backed innovation programmes also qualify.

Skilled Professionals

The skilled professional category covers a broad range of specialised roles. Qualifying professions typically include doctors and healthcare specialists, engineers, scientists, legal professionals, IT and technology experts, business executives, and individuals in the fields of culture, art, and sports with recognised achievements. A valid UAE employment contract and professional licence are required.

Outstanding Students

Students who graduate from UAE universities with a GPA of 3.75 or above, or students who graduate from internationally recognised universities in the top 100 globally, can apply for the UAE Golden Visa. This category is designed to retain talent educated in the UAE and attract globally educated professionals to the country.

Family Members

All UAE Golden Visa holders can sponsor their spouse, children (regardless of age), and parents for UAE residency under the same 10-year validity. This means the entire family can establish long-term, stable residency in the UAE without employer dependency.

Documents Required for UAE Golden Visa

The documents required depend on the eligibility category. Here is a complete breakdown of what is needed for each category:

For All Applicants:

  • Valid passport copy — minimum 6 months validity, all data pages
  • Current UAE Emirates ID copy
  • Valid UAE residence visa copy (current sponsor)
  • Passport-size photograph — white background, recent
  • UAE health insurance certificate

For Investors (Additional):

  • Title deed or property ownership certificate — showing AED 2M+ value
  • Mortgage statement (if property is mortgaged — showing AED 2M+ paid)
  • Bank statements confirming investment value
  • Letter from DLD (Dubai Land Department) or relevant authority confirming investment qualification

For Skilled Professionals (Additional):

  • Valid UAE employment contract or work permit
  • Salary certificate — confirming salary above qualifying threshold
  • Professional licence or syndicate membership (for doctors, engineers, lawyers)
  • Educational degree certificates — attested

For Entrepreneurs (Additional):

  • UAE trade licence copy — valid and current
  • Audited financial statements or revenue proof
  • Business incubator or accelerator endorsement letter (if applicable)
  • Memorandum of Association (MOA)

For Outstanding Students (Additional):

  • University graduation certificate with GPA details
  • Transcript of records — officially stamped by the university
  • University ranking confirmation (for international universities)

UAE Golden Visa Renewal — What You Need to Know

The UAE Golden Visa is valid for 10 years and is renewable. Renewal requires the holder to still meet the original eligibility criteria — an investor must still hold the qualifying investment, a professional must still be employed or self-employed in the qualifying role.

Renewal documents mirror the original application — updated passport, Emirates ID, proof of continued qualifying investment or employment, and updated health insurance. TamkinExpress tracks renewal dates for all Golden Visa holders and initiates the process proactively, well before the expiry date.

Family members sponsored under a Golden Visa holder's residency must also be renewed when the primary holder renews. TamkinExpress handles all family member renewals simultaneously to ensure no gaps in residency status.

Important: The UAE Golden Visa does not automatically renew — the holder must initiate the renewal process. TamkinExpress recommends beginning the renewal process at least 3 months before expiry to allow time for any document updates or authority queries.

Common Mistakes in Golden Visa Applications

Even well-prepared Golden Visa applications are sometimes delayed or rejected due to avoidable errors. These are the most common issues TamkinExpress encounters and prevents for clients:

  • Applying under the wrong category — some applicants apply as investors when they qualify as professionals, or vice versa. The wrong category leads to rejection even if the documents are correct.
  • Unattested educational certificates — for skilled professionals, degrees must be attested through the correct multi-step process before they are accepted by UAE authorities.
  • Salary below the qualifying threshold — skilled professionals must confirm that their current UAE salary meets the minimum requirement for their professional category.
  • Investment property below AED 2M valuation — the property must be independently valued at AED 2M or above at the time of application. Outdated valuations are rejected.
  • Missing nomination letter — skilled professionals require a formal nomination letter from their employer or professional authority. Applications submitted without this are incomplete.
