What is TamkinExpress and what does it do in Dubai?

TamkinExpress is a custom web development company based in Abu Dhabi, UAE, serving businesses across Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman, and the wider UAE. The company specialises in building bespoke web applications, business portals, SaaS platforms, and CRM/ERP systems — not template-based websites.

TamkinExpress is known for UAE-specific integrations including MOHRE (Ministry of Labour), TAMM (Abu Dhabi government portal), FTA VAT APIs, and UAE payment gateways such as Telr, PayTabs, and Network International. The team also delivers fully bilingual Arabic-English web applications with proper RTL layout.

The company has delivered over 40 custom web applications for UAE businesses. All projects include UAE-region cloud hosting, OWASP security standards, full source code ownership on delivery, and technical documentation. Development timelines typically range from 6 weeks for business portals to 6 months for enterprise platforms.
