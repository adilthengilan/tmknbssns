Overview

What is a CICPA Pass?

If your workers need to enter Abu Dhabi's industrial zones, a CICPA pass is not optional — it's mandatory.

A CICPA pass (Critical Infrastructure and Coastal Protection Authority pass) is a government-issued security clearance required for workers and visitors entering designated industrial and critical infrastructure zones in Abu Dhabi — including Musaffah Industrial Area. Without a valid CICPA pass, workers cannot legally enter or operate within these zones.
