Sharjah Business Landscape

Why Set Up a Business
in Sharjah, UAE?

Sharjah is the UAE's third-largest emirate by area and holds the distinction of being a UNESCO-designated City of Culture. Beyond its cultural identity, Sharjah is a formidable commercial and industrial force — home to one of the UAE's largest manufacturing sectors, significant logistics infrastructure, a major international airport, and two ports on both the Arabian Gulf and Gulf of Oman coasts. This dual-coast geography gives Sharjah-based businesses access to global trade routes that no other UAE emirate can match.

The Sharjah Economic Development Department (SEDD) is the emirate-level authority responsible for issuing trade licenses and regulating commercial activity across Sharjah's mainland. SEDD works in parallel with the federal Ministry of Economy UAE, which governs commercial registration, trademark protection, and national-level business approvals. Most Sharjah mainland businesses require compliance with both authorities, and Tamkin Express coordinates both simultaneously to eliminate unnecessary delays in your setup timeline.

Sharjah's industrial zones — including Hamriyah, Sajaa, and the Sharjah Airport International Free Zone (SAIF Zone) — are major anchors for manufacturing, light industry, and trading businesses. However, mainland Sharjah remains attractive for businesses that need direct access to the domestic UAE market without free zone restrictions. Tamkin Express advises on the most advantageous structure for your specific business activity between mainland, free zone, and offshore options.

Sharjah's real estate and operating costs sit notably below those of Dubai and Abu Dhabi, making it an attractive base for cost-conscious businesses that still need access to the broader UAE market. Its proximity to Dubai — just a 20-minute drive from central Sharjah to central Dubai — means that many entrepreneurs choose Sharjah as their licensing jurisdiction while operating actively across both emirates. Tamkin Express frequently assists businesses in this cross-emirate setup.

The education and healthcare sectors are particularly prominent in Sharjah. The emirate hosts the University of Sharjah, the American University of Sharjah, and numerous hospitals and medical centres. Businesses supporting these sectors — from educational technology to medical equipment supply — require specific Ministry of Economy and SEDD licensing categories that Tamkin Express is experienced in navigating. Sharjah's media sector is also significant, with the Sharjah Media City (Shams) free zone operating alongside mainland media business licensing through SEDD.

Following the UAE's 2021 foreign ownership reforms, many business categories on the Sharjah mainland now permit 100% foreign ownership. Tamkin Express advises every Sharjah client on the ownership structure options available for their specific activity, ensuring your business is structured for both current compliance and future growth. Document Checklist — Sharjah

Documents Required for
Sharjah Business Registration

Accurate, current documentation is the most critical factor in achieving first-time SEDD and Ministry of Economy approval for your Sharjah trade license.

✓
Passport CopiesValid passport copies for all shareholders and company managers. UAE-resident shareholders also require a copy of their current residence visa page.
✓
Emirates IDCurrent Emirates ID for all UAE-resident shareholders and directors. Non-resident shareholders require a notarised passport copy with UAE entry stamp where applicable.
✓
Trade Name Approval — SEDDAn approved trade name reservation certificate from the Sharjah Economic Development Department. Names must comply with UAE naming conventions and must be unique within the SEDD registry.
✓
Memorandum of AssociationA legally drafted and notarised MOA specifying shareholder details, ownership percentages, authorised capital, and the permitted scope of business activities — attested by a UAE Notary Public.
✓
Sharjah Tenancy ContractA valid tenancy contract for a Sharjah business address appropriate to your licensed activity. The premises must match the activity type — commercial, industrial, or professional — as required by SEDD.
✓
No Objection Certificate (NOC)Required if any shareholder or manager is currently sponsored by a UAE employer — ensuring compliance with UAE labour and residency regulations before SEDD and Ministry of Economy submission.
✓
Initial Approval — SEDD & MinistryInitial approvals from SEDD and, where required, the Ministry of Economy must be obtained before final license issuance. Tamkin Express manages this step on your behalf to prevent delays.
✓
Sector-Specific Sharjah ApprovalsEducation businesses require SPEA approval; healthcare requires SHA approval; media requires NMC clearance; industrial requires municipality clearance. Tamkin Express identifies and coordinates every required sector approval.
